OpenAI expanded ChatGPT Ads to seven new markets — Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, and Taiwan — following earlier Asia-Pacific rollouts in Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, and India.

## Details

- **Scale so far**: ChatGPT Ads reached a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate in under 200 days as of late August, and the platform now operates in over 60 countries with tens of thousands of active advertisers
- **Who sees ads**: only Free and Go plan users; paid subscriptions remain ad-free
- **Privacy stance**: ads are always clearly labeled and separate from ChatGPT's answers, user conversations stay private from advertisers, no customer data is sold, and users can control ad personalization preferences
- **Access paths**: OpenAI's own Ads Manager, agency partners including dentsu, Havas Media, Omnicom, Publicis, and WPP, or direct outreach to the OpenAI Ads Solutions team

## What happened next

The expansion into seven Southeast Asian markets plus Taiwan continues ChatGPT Ads' rapid geographic scaling since its $1 billion run-rate milestone, following the Sponsored Agents pilot and HubSpot/Shopify integrations announced earlier in September.
