GitHub will deprecate the following models on December 14, 2026 across all Copilot experiences: Copilot Chat, inline edits, ask and agent modes, and code completions.

## Models and suggested alternatives

| Model | Suggested alternative |
|---|---|
| Claude Fable 5 | Claude Fable 5.2 |
| Claude Haiku 4.5 (*) | Claude Haiku 5.5 |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash | Gemini 4 Hydrogen |
| Gemini 4 Argon | Gemini 4 Carbon |
| Grok 4.6 | Grok 4.7 |
| Kimi K3 | To be announced |

(*) Claude Haiku 4.5 stays available to individual Copilot subscribers on annual plans.

## Details

- **Default enablement:** the suggested alternatives are enabled automatically for Copilot Enterprise and Business customers, unless an administrator has turned off the global default or disabled the model.
- **If the global default is off:** admins can enable the alternatives through model policies in Copilot settings.
- **Action:** update workflows and integrations to supported models before the date. No action is needed to remove the models afterward.
