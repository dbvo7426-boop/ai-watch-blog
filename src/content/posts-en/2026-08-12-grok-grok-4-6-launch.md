xAI released Grok 4.6 on August 12, 2026, positioning it as a flagship model built for "long-running agents and more ambitious interactive and visual work." The model builds on Grok 4.5 with a particular focus on staying on task across many steps, whether researching a topic, working across a codebase, or turning a broad product idea into a working first version of an application.

## Details

- **Focus on long-horizon agentic work**: xAI says Grok 4.6 shows stronger performance on multi-step tasks across research, coding, and application development, with improved self-testing and verification as tasks get longer
- **Visual and interactive projects**: the company highlights "stronger first passes on visual and interactive projects than we typically saw with Grok 4.5"
- **Benchmarks**: Grok 4.6 scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, matching OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol and closing to within one point of Anthropic's Claude Fable 5; other reported scores include CursorBench v3.2 at 69.9%, DeepSWE v1.1 at 65.9%, and AA-Briefcase at 1577
- **Safety**: xAI says safeguards were recalibrated to match the model's expanded capabilities
- **Pricing**: $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, with a "fast" variant available at double the price
- **Launch promotion**: xAI is offering 2x included usage for the first week after launch

## How to try it

- Available now in Cursor and in xAI's own Grok Build tool
- Available via the xAI API, and through partners including OpenRouter, Vercel, and Cloudflare
- Start at x.ai/build or through the API console
