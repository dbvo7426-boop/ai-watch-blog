Lovable added a connector for Parallel on September 22, 2026, giving apps built on the platform a way to pull live, cited information from the web without a separate research integration.

## Details

- **Live web search and reading**: connected apps can search the live web, and read any public page or PDF as clean text
- **Cited answers**: apps can get cited answers to research questions rather than plain generated text
- **Structured deep research**: the connector supports deep research tasks that fill structured data fields with sourced information
- **Target use cases**: Lovable highlights lead enrichment, due diligence, market research, and chatbots that need answers grounded in current information
- **Setup**: users on the "Managed by Lovable" tier don't need their own Parallel account — usage is billed against workspace credits — while others can add it via Connectors or simply ask Lovable to connect it inside a project

## What happened next

The connector rounds out a busy week of Lovable changelog updates that also included a Files collections redesign on September 20 and a broader chat/admin update on September 21, extending the range of external services an app built in Lovable can call on without custom integration work.
