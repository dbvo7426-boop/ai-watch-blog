GitHub Copilot in Slack and Microsoft Teams gained multi-repository support and several other workflow improvements.

## Details

- **Multi-repo support**: Copilot can now work across multiple repositories in a single conversation, letting users ask it to list open pull requests across repos, report which PRs were merged in a week, investigate an issue spanning several repos, or summarize recent activity
- **Flexible mentions**: users can mention @GitHub anywhere in a message, not just at the start
- **Smoother repo switching**: transitions between repositories during a conversation are improved
- **Shared context**: Copilot can use shared Slack messages and threads as context, removing the need to copy details into the chat
- **Reliability**: fewer errors, faster task startup, and smoother recovery from interruptions

## What happened next

The update continues GitHub's incremental investment in chat-platform integrations for Copilot, extending the single-repo-focused experience most users had in Slack and Teams toward the same cross-repository workflows already available in Copilot's IDE and CLI surfaces.
