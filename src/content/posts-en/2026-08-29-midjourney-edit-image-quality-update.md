Midjourney published a short update on August 29, 2026 announcing that it had improved image quality on its V8.2 edit model, just two days after that edit model first rolled out. The note is brief but direct: the team asked anyone who ran into edit quality issues over the previous 24 hours to try again.

## Details

- **What changed**: Midjourney says it "updated our V8.2 edit model for better image quality," without detailing the specific technical fix
- **Who it affects**: users who tried the newly launched edit workflow (text-instructed edits, multi-reference generation, inpainting/outpainting) in the day before the patch
- **Ask to the community**: retry edits that previously looked off and report back on whether the fix resolved the issue
- **More to come**: the update closes with a note that "more updates soon" are planned for the edit model

## What happened next

This is a fast follow-up to the V8.2 edit model's August 27 debut, consistent with Midjourney's pattern of shipping a new feature broadly, then quickly iterating on quality once real-world usage surfaces rough edges. No new capabilities were added in this patch — it's purely a quality fix — and Midjourney has signaled further edit-model updates are still coming.
