Google launched Skills in Gemini, reusable custom instructions users can save once and invoke repeatedly instead of retyping the same prompts.

## Details

- **How they work**: create a skill once, then invoke it by typing "/" followed by its name in the prompt bar; skills can be stacked together for more complex workflows and now support reference files including text documents, PDFs, and images
- **Example uses**: drafting presentations from a custom outline and talking points, matching a user's personal writing style across documents, generating multiple perspectives on a topic for brainstorming, and combining multiple skills for on-brand content
- **Availability**: rolling out globally in Gemini chat across all Google AI subscription tiers now, with Workspace customers (business, enterprise, nonprofit, and education) getting access in the coming weeks
- **Replacing Gems**: Skills are replacing Gems as Gemini's primary customization tool; Gems will be phased out in November for personal accounts, March 2027 for Workspace business/enterprise/nonprofit customers, and June 2027 for education customers, with Google automatically migrating existing Gems into skills during the transition

## What happened next

The Gems-to-Skills transition mirrors a broader industry shift toward composable, file-aware prompt automation, arriving the same week OpenAI pushed deeper into persistent automation with its "dots" agents — both companies converging on the idea that repetitive prompting, not raw model capability, is the next friction point to remove.
