OpenAI named Aleph its exclusive reseller for ChatGPT Ads in 44 markets, effective November 12, 2026. Aleph offers local contracting and invoicing in local currency, payment terms, onboarding, campaign support and training.

- Advertisers can still use the self-service ChatGPT Ads Manager or OpenAI's global technology partners, and can still target any country where ChatGPT Ads are available.
- Markets include 14 in Latin America (e.g. Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Peru), 16 in Europe and Central Asia (e.g. Bulgaria, Czechia, Hungary, Romania), 10 in the Middle East and Africa (e.g. Egypt, Kenya, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, UAE) and 4 in Asia-Pacific (Bangladesh, Cambodia, Hong Kong, Sri Lanka).
- ChatGPT is not available in mainland China or Hong Kong, so ads do not run in-market there; businesses there can advertise to users elsewhere through authorized partners.
- OpenAI says over 100,000 marketers have advertised on ChatGPT and plans new markets, formats, optimization tools and measurement solutions.
