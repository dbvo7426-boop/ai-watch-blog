OpenAI launched a new visual ad format in ChatGPT, shown during image generation, alongside a significantly expanded set of measurement and attribution partnerships for advertisers.

## Details

- **The format**: a visual ad showing product inspiration, usage scenarios, and lifestyle imagery, letting advertisers demonstrate how a product fits into users' lives through pictures rather than text alone; ads appear during image generation in ChatGPT for users on the Free and Go plans
- **Separation from content**: ads are clearly labeled and kept visually separate from generated images, and OpenAI states advertising does not alter ChatGPT's responses
- **Data and attribution partners**: Hightouch, Tealium, and LiveRamp let advertisers connect existing conversion data; AppsFlyer, Adjust, DV Rockerbox, Northbeam, Branch, and others support conversion tracking and click attribution
- **Incrementality measurement**: partnerships with Haus, Measured, and WorkMagic let advertisers assess the causal impact of ChatGPT Ads through geo-based testing
- **Early results cited**: WeightWatchers reported a 15.3% lower cost per acquisition versus paid-search benchmarks; Dose saw a statistically significant lift with 67% of incremental purchases from new customers; Portland Leather reported 93% of ChatGPT-driven visitors were new customers

## What happened next

This marks one of OpenAI's most concrete moves yet into a conventional advertising business model for ChatGPT's free tiers, built out with a measurement stack comparable to established ad platforms — a notable shift for a company whose free-tier monetization strategy had previously relied mainly on usage limits and upsells to paid plans.
