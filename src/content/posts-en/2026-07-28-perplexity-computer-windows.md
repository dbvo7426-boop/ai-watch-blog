Perplexity announced that its agent platform, Personal Computer, now supports Windows 10 and 11. Previously centered on Mac, the platform now extends to more than 1 billion Windows devices worldwide, letting users work across local files, Microsoft 365 apps, and the web from a single interface.

## Details

- **Local file management**: Create, edit, and organize Word, Excel, and other documents stored on the machine
- **Microsoft ecosystem integration**: Native integration with Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, and SharePoint
- **Broad app connectivity**: Connects to more than 400 applications via App Connectors, including Snowflake, Salesforce, and HubSpot
- **Cross-device workflow**: Users can start a task on their phone and finish it on the desktop
- **Voice control**: Voice mode enables verbal commands for PC operations
- **Web-to-local bridge**: Conducts web research and incorporates the findings directly into local documents
- **Security features**: Alerts users before sensitive actions, maintains audit trails, runs in a secure sandbox, and doesn't train on customer data
- **Availability**: Available now on Windows 10 and 11 for Pro, Max, and Enterprise subscribers

## How to try it

- Available to Perplexity Pro, Max, or Enterprise subscribers running Windows 10 or 11
- Start by connecting Microsoft apps like Word, Excel, Outlook, Teams, and SharePoint, or one of the 400+ App Connectors such as Snowflake, Salesforce, or HubSpot
