Google announced "Expert Intelligence" for Gemini Notebook (the product formerly known as NotebookLM) on September 17, 2026, a new way to ground notebooks in trusted, published sources rather than only user-uploaded material. The feature launches first in Gemini Notebook, giving users access to more than 100,000 books from major publishers.

## Details

- **What it does**: users can add compatible ebooks purchased through Google Play Books directly into a Gemini Notebook, then ask questions with answers grounded in that book's actual text
- **Combine with personal sources**: book content can sit alongside a user's own documents, syllabi, or notes in the same notebook, letting Gemini Notebook cross-reference both
- **Generated outputs supported**: infographics, audio overviews, quizzes, and other Studio-style outputs can be built from book sources the same way they work for other material
- **Example use cases Google cites**: students pairing a course syllabus with assigned textbooks to prepare for exams, and managers consulting business books for leadership guidance
- **Sharing caveat**: if a notebook containing a book is shared, recipients must have purchased that same ebook themselves to interact with it
- **Rollout**: available now to Rapid Release and Scheduled Release Google Workspace domains, plus Workspace Individual subscribers and personal Google Account users who have Gemini Notebook, Google Play, and Google Books enabled

## What happened next

Expert Intelligence extends Gemini Notebook's core pitch — answers grounded in verifiable sources — beyond material a user personally uploads, tying it directly into Google's existing Play Books catalog and purchase flow. Google described it as the first application of a broader "Expert Intelligence" initiative intended to bring trusted third-party content into its AI products, suggesting more publisher or expert-source integrations could follow in other Google AI surfaces.
