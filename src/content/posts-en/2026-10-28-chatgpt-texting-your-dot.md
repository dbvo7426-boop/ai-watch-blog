OpenAI began rolling out Texting your dot in beta to ChatGPT Pro users in the U.S. A dot can now be reached by text message, and can also text you first.

## Details

- **Channels:** iMessage on iPhone or RCS on Android. Users enable it from the dot's profile in ChatGPT by selecting Texting.
- **Proactive messages:** the dot can text you with updates it judges relevant.
- **Context:** texting shares the same memory and plugins as the dot in the ChatGPT app.
- **Privacy:** only the user can text the dot and the dot can only text the user; OpenAI says this does not give the dot or OpenAI access to the user's other text conversations.
- **Provider:** messaging runs through third-party provider Linq; OpenAI says it ran extensive penetration testing with frontier models and third-party security firms.
- **Availability:** beta, Pro plan, U.S. only. Wider rollout: undisclosed.
- **Also noted the same day:** eligible Pro and Business subscribers can activate promotional API credits, separate from ChatGPT usage credits.

## What happened next

Moving a dot into the messaging apps puts ChatGPT on the surface people already check, and proactive texting is a step beyond chat-initiated use. The reliance on a third-party messaging provider is the main trust consideration; plans beyond Pro and regions beyond the U.S. have not been announced.
