OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Financial Services, a specialized version of ChatGPT built for investment banking and equity research teams, combining built-in market data with GPT-6 Astra's reasoning for research, financial modeling, and client-facing materials.

## Details

- **Built-in data**: includes Daloopa, PitchBook, LSEG News, and Crunchbase, covering earnings transcripts, financial statements, company fundamentals, and private-company information
- **Subscription integrations**: automatically recognizes existing entitlements for S&P Capital IQ, LSEG, MSCI, Dow Jones Factiva, and Moody's
- **Connectors**: over 50 integrations, including Datasite, Box, Preqin, FactSet, and Intapp
- **Design partners**: developed with Morgan Stanley and Evercore ahead of launch
- **Core features**: citation tracking that lets users trace figures and claims back to their sources, GPT-6 Astra-powered document analysis and complex table interpretation, interactive chart and visualization creation, and firm-template customization for Excel, Word, and PowerPoint outputs
- **Security and governance**: SAML SSO, encryption at rest and in transit, configurable data retention, compliance audit logging, and role-based access controls
- **Availability**: exclusive to eligible financial institutions through direct inquiry or an OpenAI account team; no public pricing disclosed

## What happened next

The launch extends OpenAI's pattern of building industry-specific ChatGPT products — following healthcare and education — into one of the most data- and compliance-intensive verticals, with the Morgan Stanley and Evercore partnerships positioning it to compete directly with existing financial-data and workflow tools built into Bloomberg terminals and similar platforms.
