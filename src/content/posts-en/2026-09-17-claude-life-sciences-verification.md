Anthropic launched the Life Sciences Verification Program (LSVP), giving vetted life sciences professionals access to its Mythos, Opus, and Sonnet models under safeguards tailored to biology research, replacing real-time blocking with offline monitoring to reduce interruptions to legitimate work.

## Details

- **Who it's for**: academic laboratories, startups, pharmaceutical companies, and research institutions working in drug discovery, research biology, clinical development, manufacturing, supply chain, quality assurance, regulatory affairs, and investing
- **Verification process**: applicants are reviewed on research credentials, security standards, and ethical research oversight, and must describe intended use cases at a high level — similar to a job posting — without sensitive information or IP
- **Two access tiers**: Standard Use covers most life science work, is renewed annually, and is available to entire teams; High-risk Use is a project-specific add-on requiring additional vetting, renewed every six months
- **Monitoring approach**: usage is checked against stated use cases with 30-day data retention on flagged activity; data is compartmentalized and excluded from model training or Anthropic's own life sciences research
- **Partner organizations**: Xaira, Edison, and Manifold Bio are quoted supporting the program
- **Availability**: accessible now via the API, Claude Science, Claude for Enterprise, and Team plans — not yet available on individual plans or third-party platforms, and organizations with a Business Associate Agreement are excluded during the beta

## What happened next

The program addresses a specific tension in AI safety for dual-use biology research — safeguards designed to block bioweapon-relevant queries can also slow down legitimate drug discovery work — by shifting from blocking suspicious requests outright to reviewing flagged activity after the fact for verified, credentialed researchers.
