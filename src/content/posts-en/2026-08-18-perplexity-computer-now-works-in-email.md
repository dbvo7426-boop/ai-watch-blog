Perplexity's Computer agent platform can now be invoked directly from email, letting users kick off full agent tasks by sending, forwarding, or cc'ing `computer@perplexity.com` without switching to a separate app.

## Details

- **Three ways to trigger it**: Write a new email describing the task, forward an existing thread, or cc Computer into an ongoing conversation
- **Verified, permission-scoped execution**: Computer verifies the sender, reads the full thread and any attachments, and runs the task using that person's own access permissions and connectors — it doesn't get broader access than the sender already has
- **Example tasks**: Building financial models such as LBO analyses or DCFs as Excel files, summarizing legal document revisions and tracking open issues, processing email attachments, and producing formatted deliverables (Excel, PDFs, presentations)
- **Uses accumulated memory**: Computer draws on its memory of prior sessions to apply a team's terminology, formats, and past decisions when completing email-triggered tasks
- **Output**: Results post directly back into the email thread, with complex deliverables attached as files; every task also creates a full session viewable on Computer's mobile and web apps
- **Availability**: Live today for all Computer users; reply-all support for Enterprise customers is coming

## How to try it

- Send a new email to `computer@perplexity.com` describing a task, or forward/cc it into an existing thread
- Check the mobile or web Computer app afterward to review the full session behind any emailed result
