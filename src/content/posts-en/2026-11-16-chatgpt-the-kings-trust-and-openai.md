The King's Trust and OpenAI launched a partnership to help young people across the UK build AI skills for work, prioritizing those not in education, employment or training (NEET).

- OpenAI is donating 1 million pounds to the youth charity.
- The program aims to support up to 25,000 young people over the next 18 months, combining AI skills learning with recognized qualifications and practical experience.
- It forms part of a wider program of nationally recognized AI qualifications that The King's Trust will launch in the coming months. Details of the curriculum and the qualifications are undisclosed.
- OpenAI points to OpenAI Academy, which offers free learning resources, as related work.
