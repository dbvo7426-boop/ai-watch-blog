Google is rolling out 66 new regional voices in the Gemini app on Android and iOS, announced on November 16, 2026.

- Languages and variants: U.S. English, U.K. English, India English, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian and Korean.
- The voices aim for natural, everyday delivery rather than exaggerated accents.
- The chosen voice is used across the app, including Gemini Live, language practice and the Listen icon in chat.
- To preview and select, tap the three-dot menu in Gemini Live or open Gemini's voice in app settings.
- The per-language breakdown of the 66 voices, country availability and a rollout schedule beyond rolling out are undisclosed.
