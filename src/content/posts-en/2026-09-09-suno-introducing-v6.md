Suno CEO Mikey Shulman announced v6 on September 9, 2026, a new generation of music-creation models developed in partnership with Warner Music Group, BMG, and Believe, described as faster, more expressive, and higher quality than prior versions.

## Details

- **Three model variants**: v6 (flagship, for Pro/Premier subscribers, tuned for precision and reliability across genres), v6-wild (Pro/Premier, favors unpredictable and experimental results), and v6-mini (faster and free to all users, positioned as outperforming competing free platforms)
- **Natural-language editing**: users can edit specific song sections with text instructions while leaving the rest of the track untouched, and modify individual lyric lines without regenerating the whole song
- **Mashups and sampling**: the models can combine multiple source tracks into a mashup in one request, and sample or isolate audio elements to build new compositions
- **Multi-input generation**: songs can be generated from emotional descriptions, visual references, or a mix of text, audio, image, and video inputs
- **Deeper musical understanding**: Suno says the models now better comprehend musical terminology covering vocals, instrumentation, structure, mood, reference tracks, and overall sonic character
- **Rollout**: previous model versions will be retired as v6 rolls out platform-wide
- **Future plans**: Suno says it is working on opt-in, artist-focused experiences that would let creators earn compensation from the new models

## What happened next

v6 rolls out across Suno's platform as older models are phased out, with v6-mini giving free-tier users immediate access to the upgraded architecture. The launch arrives one day after Suno's distribution partnership with Believe and TuneCore, and follows a string of major-label tie-ups (BMG in August, Warner Music Group last November) that together suggest Suno is positioning v6 less as a standalone tool and more as the technical backbone of a broader, industry-endorsed AI music ecosystem.
