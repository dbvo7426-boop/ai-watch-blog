Sakana AI has been awarded a contract by Japan's Ministry of Defense to research and demonstrate AI capabilities for the ministry's "comprehensive analysis operations," applying its AI agent technology to strategic intelligence work.

## Details

- **Awarding body**: Japan's Ministry of Defense (防衛省), specifically its Information Bureau
- **Contract date**: July 29, 2026
- **Contract value**: Not disclosed
- **Scope**: Applying advanced AI agent technology to strengthen intelligence analysis operations, targeting three areas — efficiency improvements in information gathering, enhanced analytical capabilities, and more systematic information management
- **What "comprehensive analysis operations" means**: Strategic intelligence work that supports national security policy decisions, distinct from tactical command-and-control systems. The goal is to help analysts handle information overload and expand analytical capacity through AI-driven speed, accuracy, and efficiency
- **Status**: Currently in an investigation-and-demonstration phase, not a full deployment
- **Related prior work**: In March 2026, Sakana AI separately contracted with Japan's Defense Acquisition, Technology & Logistics Agency (ATLA) on research to advance command-and-control systems

## What happened next

This is Sakana AI's second known engagement with Japan's defense establishment in 2026, following the March ATLA contract, suggesting a deepening relationship between the Japanese AI startup and the country's national security apparatus. As the demonstration project is still in its early phase, further details on scope, cost, and eventual deployment are expected to follow as the work progresses.
