Google DeepMind announced research on Genie 4, a new world model for real-time interactive simulation. Google says it adds more realistic visuals and native audio.

## Details

- **Built on Gemini:** Gemini Omni anchors simulations in real-world appearance and behavior, and other Gemini models provide the reasoning that keeps a simulation coherent and continuous.
- **Real-time steering:** you can set an event in motion and steer scenes in natural language, such as moving the camera, changing the weather or introducing new characters.
- **Native audio:** every world generates audio on the fly, including ambient sounds, music and interactive dialogue.
- **Intended uses:** Google points to safe training environments for robots and agents, and creative tools for filmmaking, journalism, gaming and manufacturing.
- **Availability:** rolling out to a limited group of trusted testers. Google says it is coming soon to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and to early access partners in the Gemini API and Google AI Studio. Exact dates, pricing and technical specs (resolution, frame rate, session length) were not disclosed in the announcement.
