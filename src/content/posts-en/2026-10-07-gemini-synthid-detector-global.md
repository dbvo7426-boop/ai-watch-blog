Google DeepMind announced on October 7, 2026 that SynthID Detector is available globally in English. The post, by Pushmeet Kohli, was updated on October 29, 2026.

## Details

- **Who can use it:** Everyone. It expands the earlier version built for media professionals to general public access.
- **What it checks:** Whether an image, video or audio file was made with AI from Google or its partners. The partners named include OpenAI, NVIDIA, Kakao and Apple, introduced with the word including, so the list may not be exhaustive.
- **Access:** Through the SynthID Detector at synthid.com.
- **Related tools:** Built-in verification already exists in Search, the Gemini app and Chrome, which Google says regularly handle over 1 million requests daily.
- **Scope note:** The detector only recognizes media from companies that use SynthID. Usage limits, quotas and pricing are not disclosed in the post.
