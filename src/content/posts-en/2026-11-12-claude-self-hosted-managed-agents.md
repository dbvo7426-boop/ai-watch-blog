Anthropic's release notes for November 12, 2026 list the launch of self-hosted Claude Managed Agents.

## Details

- **What you run:** Customers run the whole Managed Agents service on their own infrastructure. This includes storing agents, sessions and session history.
- **Model requests:** Requests go to the provider the customer chooses. Anthropic is the default.
- **Access:** Access requires contacting your Anthropic account team. Pricing and general availability dates are not disclosed in the release notes.

## Why it matters

Teams that need agent state and conversation history to stay inside their own environment can now keep that data in-house while still using Claude Managed Agents.
