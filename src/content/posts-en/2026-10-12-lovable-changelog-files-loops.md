Lovable's October 12, 2026 changelog adds lightweight image editing inside the Files tab, clearer visibility into free-allowance usage, and a new connector for the Loops email and marketing automation platform.

## Details

- **Edit images in the Files tab**: users can draw over an area, pin comments, erase content, or remove a background directly on an uploaded image; Lovable saves the result as a new file
- **Free-allowance coverage breakdown**: the Usage details view now shows how much of a workspace's usage was covered by free allowances, via a new "By coverage" breakdown
- **Loops connector**: apps can manage contacts, send events that trigger Loops automations, and send transactional email through the new connector

## What happened next

These are incremental quality-of-life updates rather than headline features, but they follow a pattern in Lovable's recent changelogs of pairing new third-party connectors with small usability and billing-transparency improvements — in this case making it easier to see what free credits actually cover, and giving builders a lightweight way to touch up uploaded images without leaving the Files tab.
