Google launched a dedicated Gemini desktop app for Windows, designed to sit alongside a user's existing tools rather than requiring a browser tab or separate workflow.

## Details

- **Instant access**: pressing Alt+Space opens Gemini over whatever application is currently active, without switching windows
- **Delegated tasks**: users can hand off complex tasks to Gemini Spark, an AI agent, or request summaries that pull in context from Gmail and Drive
- **Creative tools**: supports image and video generation directly from the desktop using Nano Banana and Gemini Omni
- **Performance**: described by Google as lightweight and quiet, built not to slow down the user's PC
- **Availability**: available globally now for Windows 10 and 11, downloadable from gemini.google/desktop

## What happened next

A native, always-accessible desktop app gives Gemini a permanent presence on Windows PCs comparable to dedicated assistant apps from other AI vendors, positioning quick keyboard-shortcut access as Google's answer to browser-tab-based AI usage.
