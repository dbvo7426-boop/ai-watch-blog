The company behind the general-purpose AI agent Manus has announced it is joining Meta, in a deal reported to be worth more than $2 billion. The acquisition caps a period of rapid growth for Manus, which will continue to operate independently out of Singapore.

## Details

- **Announcement**: Announced December 29, 2025, on the official Manus blog. The company becomes part of the Meta group
- **Track record so far**: Manus's agent has processed more than 147 trillion tokens, powered the creation of over 80 million virtual computers, and served millions of users worldwide
- **Leadership comment**: Manus CEO Xiao Hong said, "Joining Meta allows us to build on a stronger, more sustainable foundation without changing how Manus works or how decisions are made"
- **Continuity**: The existing subscription service continues through the app and website without disruption, headquarters remain in Singapore, and existing customers are not expected to see any immediate changes
- **Looking ahead**: Under Meta, Manus is positioned as an "execution layer," with plans to eventually extend the product to millions of businesses and billions of people across Meta's platforms

## How to try it

- Manus remains available as before via its app and website; no immediate service changes have been announced as a result of the acquisition
- Some deal terms, including the exact purchase price, have not been officially disclosed — media reports place it in the $2-3 billion range
- Reports also indicate Meta is severing ownership ties with Manus's Chinese investors and winding down the company's China-based operations
