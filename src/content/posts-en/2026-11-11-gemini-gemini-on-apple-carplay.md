Google is bringing the Gemini app to Apple CarPlay, so iOS users can hold hands-free conversations with Gemini Live from their car's display. It follows the rollout on Android Auto and cars with Google built-in.

## What you can do

- **Resume or start chats:** tap the Gemini icon on the CarPlay screen or side rail.
- **Natural back-and-forth:** interrupt, add details, change topics or pause when you need to focus on driving.
- **Daily brief and inbox help:** with Gmail and Google Calendar connected, Gemini can give a daily brief, catch you up on unread email, summarize long threads and help draft a voice-dictated reply. Daily Brief must be enabled first.
- **Calendar:** ask what is on your day, when to leave for an appointment, or get help preparing for a meeting.
- **Local recommendations:** nearby places such as lunch spots, or background on landmarks.
- **Brainstorming and rehearsal:** practice presentations, prepare for hard conversations or have complex topics explained.
- **Sync:** CarPlay conversations sync to the Gemini app on your iPhone.

## Availability and requirements

- Rolling out globally from November 11, 2026, to all Gemini users on iOS, in every language Gemini Live currently supports.
- Requires the latest Gemini app, microphone permission, and having used Gemini Live on the phone at least once.
- Personalization features, including Daily Brief, are not available to users under 18 or in the EEA and Nigeria.
