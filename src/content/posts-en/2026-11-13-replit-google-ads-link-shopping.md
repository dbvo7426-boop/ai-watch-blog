Replit's updates page for November 13, 2026 lists several Agent and workspace additions.

## Details

- **Google Ads:** Agent can build campaigns with ad copy, images and conversion tracking. You can use an existing account with separate billing or fund a Replit-managed one with Replit credits. Managed accounts need Replit Pro, a personal Workspace and auto-reload. You approve the campaign and spending before ads run.
- **Agent and Link shopping:** Agent can compare products and prepare checkout. When you are ready to buy, you connect Link, Stripe's digital wallet, and approve the merchant, total and any recurring terms. Connecting the wallet alone does not authorize spending.
- **Performance issue detection:** Performance Agent scans your code and available logs for potential slowdowns. Run it from Monitoring, then review and test fixes before publishing. Scans need a paid plan and edit access, and they work before you publish.
- **Sharing with individual Viewers:** admins and members can share a project with specific Viewers, without giving access to the whole Viewer group.
- **Microsoft Fabric:** the integration now supports database-backed apps that create, read, update and delete records, along with server-side functions and analytics. The upgraded template is available to all Fabric-enabled customers.
- **Pricing for ad spend and other plans:** undisclosed beyond the requirements above.
