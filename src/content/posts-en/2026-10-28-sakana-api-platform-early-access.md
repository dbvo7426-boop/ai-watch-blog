Sakana AI announced the Sakana API Platform on October 28, 2026 and opened an Early Access program for enterprise customers. It lets organizations call Sakana's LLMs through an API together with management features.

## Details

- **OpenAI-compatible API:** applications that already call an LLM API only need to change the endpoint.
- **Models:** Sakana Fugu and Sakana Fugu Ultra are available first. Sakana Namazu and Sakana Fugu Max will follow. Until now, Fugu and Namazu were offered as standalone APIs.
- **Fugu:** an orchestration foundation model that combines multiple frontier models, building on research accepted at ICLR 2026 (Trinity and Conductor). The lineup is Fugu, Fugu Ultra and Fugu Max.
- **Namazu:** an LLM specialized in Japanese.
- **Management:** organization and workspace management through an admin console (members, API keys and usage in one place), role-based access control, audit logs with CSV download, and terms stating inputs are not used for training.
- **Commercial terms:** budget limits, invoice payment, MSA and DPA are listed. Pricing and terms of use are undisclosed and will be shared with applicants individually after review.
- **Roadmap:** more granular permission controls at the organization level are planned.

## What happened next

Access is granted to applicants as they become ready, so general availability and pricing remain undisclosed. The platform packages Sakana's existing models with the governance features enterprise buyers usually require, a step from research-led model releases toward a managed commercial offering.
