OpenAI announced that AI safety researcher Paul Christiano is joining its Foundation Board as a non-voting observer and member of the Safety and Security Committee, working alongside committee chair Zico Kolter.

## Details

- **Christiano's background**: he led alignment research at OpenAI from 2017 to 2021, contributing foundational work on reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF); he later founded the Alignment Research Center, a nonprofit focused on aligning advanced AI with human interests, and currently serves as Senior Technical Advisor at NIST's Center for AI Standards and Innovation
- **His new role**: non-voting observer on the OpenAI Foundation Board, sitting on the Safety and Security Committee rather than holding a voting board seat
- **OpenAI's stated rationale**: the company says the appointment brings "a distinct and technically grounded perspective" to its governance, citing his willingness to "challenge prevailing assumptions" about industry safeguards
- **His outlook**: Christiano is known for believing advanced AI could pose catastrophic risks and that developers should prepare for them proactively, combining independent alignment research experience with direct government experience evaluating frontier models

## What happened next

The appointment lands the same week as GPT-6 Astra's launch and its Critical-level cybersecurity capability designation, positioning an outside safety researcher with a track record of independent, sometimes critical analysis inside OpenAI's own governance structure as its models grow more capable.
