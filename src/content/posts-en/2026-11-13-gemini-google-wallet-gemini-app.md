Google announced that Gemini can connect to Google Wallet through Personal Intelligence, so its answers can draw on your spending habits.

## Details

- **Availability:** rolling out to Gemini app users in the US, on web and mobile (Android and iOS). Other countries, pricing and plans are not mentioned.
- **Optional bank linking:** users can also link credit card and bank accounts to Wallet through Plaid for deeper insights.
- **Example uses:** visualizing spending and budgets with charts, finding forgotten subscriptions and price increases, and suggesting a holiday budget based on last year's gift and travel spending.
- **Privacy and control:** Gemini can only read information from connected accounts and cannot move, manage or invest money. The connection is opt-in and can be switched on or off under Connected Apps in Personal Intelligence settings.
- **Memory Summary:** a related feature shows what Gemini remembers about your preferences, interests and goals, and lets you review, edit or remove them. It is available on web and mobile wherever Personal Intelligence is available.
