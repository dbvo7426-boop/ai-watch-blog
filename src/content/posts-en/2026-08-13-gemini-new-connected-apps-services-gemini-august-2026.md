Google has expanded the roster of third-party apps and services that can be connected to the Gemini app, rolling out new integrations across productivity, local services, music, and home/health categories over the following weeks.

## Details

- **Productivity and creativity**: Granola and Otter.ai connect for meeting summaries, and Wix connects for website editing directly from Gemini
- **Local and entertainment services**: Fever, GetYourGuide, Localiza, OpenTable (UK), and Ticketmaster let Gemini search for and book experiences, car rentals, restaurant reservations, and event tickets
- **Music**: iHeartRadio and Pandora add station discovery and playlist streaming
- **Home, health, and lifestyle**: Angi and Thumbtack connect users with home service professionals, and Zocdoc enables booking doctor appointments
- **How Google frames it**: The announcement's key line is, "By turning on your favorite services in Gemini, you can easily plan, create, and tackle your to-do list all in one place"
- **Availability details are light**: Google's post doesn't specify regional restrictions, subscription-tier limits, or device compatibility for the new connections, pointing users to a support page for more information instead

## How to try it

Users can turn on any of the newly supported apps and services from Gemini's connected apps settings; the rollout is happening gradually over the coming weeks, so availability may vary by account and region in the meantime.
