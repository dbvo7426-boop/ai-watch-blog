Google rolled out a back-to-school update for Gemini Notebook (the product formerly known as NotebookLM) on September 18, 2026, headlined by real-time voice conversations and mobile audio recording, alongside a free year of Google AI subscriptions for students.

## Details

- **Real-time voice conversations**: users 18 and older can talk through a topic with their notebook via the mobile app in nearly 100 languages, with answers grounded in the notebook's sources and support for interrupting mid-response; rolling out first to Google AI Ultra subscribers
- **Mobile lecture recording**: a new built-in recorder captures lectures or spoken notes on the go, with recordings automatically added as sources alongside a user's other material for citing and editing
- **Richer study overviews**: a new reports section combines source summaries with quizzes, flashcards, and mind maps in one view; quizzes gain new formats (short answer, multiple select, fill-in-the-blank) plus the ability to chat about wrong answers and customize question sets
- **Short Video Overviews**: roughly 60-second narrated videos with animations, generated in 80+ languages, aimed at explaining a concept quickly
- **Student pricing**: eligible US college students get one free year of Google AI Pro (a $19.99/month value, 4x the free usage limits); students in 140+ other markets get one free year of Google AI Plus; redemption runs through December 31, 2026
- **Rollout**: features are reaching Rapid Release and Scheduled Release Google Workspace domains, Workspace Individual subscribers, and personal Google Account users over the coming weeks

## What happened next

The update pairs feature expansion with a customer-acquisition push timed to the northern-hemisphere school year, following July's rebrand from NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook and last week's "Expert Intelligence" book-grounding feature. Voice conversations and lecture recording move Gemini Notebook further from a pure research tool toward an everyday study companion, with broader tier availability expected to follow the initial Ultra-subscriber rollout.
