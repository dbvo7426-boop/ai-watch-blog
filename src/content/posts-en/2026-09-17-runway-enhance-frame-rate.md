Runway added a frame rate conversion feature to its Enhance toolkit on September 17, 2026, available to subscribers on Max+ plans.

## Details

- **What it does**: converts any video — whether it was made in Runway or uploaded from elsewhere — to a target frame rate of 25, 30, 48, 60, 120, or 59.94 fps
- **Format limits**: supports source video up to 4K resolution and up to five minutes long
- **Where it's available**: accessible from the Enhance menu, Tool Mode, Workflows, and Agent
- **Access tier**: limited to Max+ plan subscribers rather than all paid tiers

## What happened next

The frame rate tool extends Runway's Enhance suite, which already handles tasks like upscaling and restoration, into more conventional post-production territory that editors would otherwise handle in traditional NLEs. Making it available through Workflows and Agent, not just the manual Enhance menu, means the conversion can be chained into automated pipelines alongside Runway's other generation and editing tools.
