OpenAI rolled out GPT-6 and a new "Intelligent UI" capability to its full base of 1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users, extending access beyond the paid tiers that received it first.

## Details

- **What Intelligent UI does**: lets ChatGPT compose responses using text, visuals, and interactive elements, choosing how they fit together based on the question, rather than limiting answers to plain text
- **Rollout schedule**: ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise got access immediately; Free and Go tiers began receiving it the following day
- **Models involved**: GPT-6 Sol powers the feature for paid tiers, while GPT-6 Luna powers it for free tiers
- **Example features**: visual plans, recipes with built-in timing, maps with route notes, interactive diagrams for learning complex topics, and built-in calculators, bill splitters, and playable games directly inside a conversation
- **Speed improvements**: GPT-6 Instant starts answering web-search queries 44% sooner on average than its predecessor; GPT-6 Extra High can begin responding in comparable time to earlier versions while delivering higher overall quality
- **Streaming behavior**: responses can progressively generate and stream to the user while the model continues thinking

## What happened next

This marks GPT-6's move from an early-access, paid-tier feature to OpenAI's default experience for its entire user base, pairing the model rollout with a visual-interface shift that pushes ChatGPT further from a plain text chatbot toward an interactive, multimodal response surface — a direction that complements OpenAI's other recent moves into computer-use agents and the "dots" proactive assistants.
