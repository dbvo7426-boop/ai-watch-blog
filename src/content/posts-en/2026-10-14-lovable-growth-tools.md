Lovable has introduced growth tools that let builders find customers for their apps from the same chat interface they use to build them, citing a survey of more than 400 builders in which two-thirds didn't know where to start marketing or had only tried posting on social media.

## Details

- **Goal-based growth plans**: users state a goal, such as their first 100 signups, and Lovable reviews the app, audience, and current traffic before proposing a plan; goals can be changed at any time
- **Google Ads integration**: Lovable can connect to or create a Google Ads account, install a Google Tag to measure conversions, and recommend campaign types across Search, YouTube, and Maps
- **Meta Ads integration**: users sign in with Facebook Login for Business to connect an ad account and Facebook Page; Lovable adds the Meta Pixel to track signups or purchases and builds image and video campaigns for Facebook and Instagram
- **Approval before spending**: Lovable summarizes budget, locations, languages, goals, and tracking before any campaign is created — nothing goes live without explicit approval, and billing runs through the user's own Google Ads or Meta account with no Lovable markup or service fee
- **Campaign management**: results surface in the Analytics tab; users can ask Lovable to pause campaigns, adjust budgets or audiences, test new copy, or build custom dashboards, though Lovable won't change live campaigns on its own
- **SEO tools**: checks for indexable pages, metadata, sitemap, robots.txt, and Google Search Console connection, with built-in Semrush data to identify rankable topics, review page performance, and generate landing pages and content
- **Off-site recommendations**: Lovable suggests relevant forums, communities, directories, and social platforms, and drafts posts and profiles for users to publish from their own accounts
- **Creative asset generation**: images, videos, product demos, video ads, landing pages, ad copy, and social posts, starting from a research report on search interest and competitors and three proposed creative directions
- **Availability**: live today on Free, Pro, and Business plans; Enterprise workspaces need an admin to enable app and chat connectors first

## What happened next

The release builds on Lovable's May 2026 Discoverability launch, which made built apps readable and indexable by search engines and AI tools, extending the platform from "build" into "build and grow." By wiring paid ad platforms, SEO data, and content generation directly into the chat-based builder — with spend requiring explicit approval and billed outside Lovable — the company is positioning itself as a full go-to-market layer for non-technical founders who ship an app but don't know how to market it.
