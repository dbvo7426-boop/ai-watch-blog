xAI announced "Grok in Google Workspace" on July 24, 2026, a free add-on that brings Grok directly into Google Sheets, Slides, and Docs. It follows the Microsoft 365 add-ins for Excel, Outlook, Word, and PowerPoint that launched the previous week, extending the same integration to Google's productivity suite.

## Details

- **In Sheets**: Ask Grok about a selected range and it answers by citing the specific cells behind its response. It can write formulas and rebuild forecasts or downside scenarios without manually reworking the sheet
- **In Slides**: Give Grok a simple outline and it researches the web and X to build a complete presentation, adding new slides that match the existing theme
- **In Docs**: Grok turns unformatted notes into a structured document with proper headings, checks grammar, and helps maintain a consistent tone across longer documents
- **External connectors**: Can pull in information from Google Drive and email via connectors
- **Availability**: A single installation from the Google Workspace Marketplace enables Grok across Sheets, Slides, and Docs. The add-on is free

## How to try it

- Search for "Grok in Google Workspace" on the Google Workspace Marketplace and install it
- No additional subscription is required to use it across Sheets, Slides, and Docs
