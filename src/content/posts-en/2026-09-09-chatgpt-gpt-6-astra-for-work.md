OpenAI published a business-focused companion post to GPT-6 Astra's launch, framing the model around dropping into existing enterprise workflows "from day one, without extensive preparation or engineering work."

## Details

- **Works with what's already there**: Astra can operate through standard business applications that lack APIs — automating tasks across Excel, analytics platforms, and other desktop tools without custom integration work
- **Professional task performance**: strong results on financial modeling, code generation, data analysis, and document production, while sticking to corporate branding standards and templates
- **New enterprise plugins**: integrations with Oracle Analytics, Power BI, Navan, and Avalara
- **Standout benchmark**: completes Excel World Cup challenges about four times faster than the competition's winning human contestant
- **Cost and coding performance**: priced at $10/$50 per million tokens, scoring 57.9% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 versus 37.3% for the prior model, at a lower effective API cost
- **Safety in practice**: produces unintended outcomes 89% less often than GPT-5.6 Sol on OpenAI's internal computer-use safety benchmarks
- **Internal validation**: OpenAI says its own engineering teams achieved 25x lower turn latency by using Astra to optimize their workflows

## What happened next

The post doubles down on administrative controls, approval workflows, and cybersecurity alignment as the differentiators meant to win over enterprise buyers, positioning Astra's business pitch around low-friction adoption rather than requiring companies to rebuild processes around it.
