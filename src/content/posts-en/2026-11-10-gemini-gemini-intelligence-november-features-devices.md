Google detailed new Gemini Intelligence features and devices on November 10, 2026. Gemini Intelligence began arriving in summer 2026 on the Pixel 11, Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Z Fold8, Z Flip8 and the S26 series.

## Devices

- **motorola signature 27:** Motorola's first Gemini Intelligence device opens for U.S. pre-order on November 19.
- **November Pixel Drop:** the Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro XL and Pixel 10 Pro Fold receive the latest Gemini Intelligence features.
- **Googlebook:** a laptop designed for Gemini Intelligence launched the previous month.

## Features

- **Rambler** (Gboard; select countries and languages; usage limits): converts spoken thoughts into organized text in real time. It is now on the Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold and motorola signature 27.
- **Task automation** (Gemini app; select countries; users 18 and over): handles tasks such as ordering food, hailing rides and booking reservations across apps. It is expanding to the UK, India and Japan.
- **Create My Widget** (select countries and languages; users 18 and over; rolling out over the next few weeks): builds a custom homescreen widget from a plain-language description, drawing on Gmail, Calendar and Search. Examples are a trip dashboard, a monthly spending tracker and a race day countdown. It is coming to Pixel Watch in the next few weeks.
- **Device assistance** (select Android devices on Android 17 or later): lets users ask Gemini to free up storage, review which apps can access location, and silence distractions while keeping important calls through.
- **Proactive suggestions on Android Auto:** coming soon to Pixel phones with Gemini Intelligence. In Google's example, Gemini finds a confirmation email about a class location and suggests sending the address with one tap.

Pricing, the exact Rambler country and language list, and a Device assistance rollout date are undisclosed.
