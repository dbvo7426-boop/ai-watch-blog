Lovable's September 15, 2026 changelog adds two new business-tool connectors and brings OpenAI's newest image models into the app, widening what builders can wire into a Lovable project without custom code.

## Details

- **WhatsApp Business integration**: Lovable apps can now connect to WhatsApp Business to receive and manage customer messages, send templated notifications, and track delivery status — aimed at support and appointment-reminder use cases
- **Power BI connector**: apps can query Power BI semantic models using DAX, letting teams surface metrics inside their app that match numbers from official company reports, with per-user access support for individual sign-ins
- **GPT Image 2.5 models**: two new OpenAI image models are now available — GPT Image 2.5 Flare for quick previews and GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst for higher-quality final assets — both supporting transparent PNG/WebP output
- **Skip behavior changed**: skipping one of Lovable's clarifying questions now only continues with defaults for the original request, instead of also approving additional suggested work
- **Workspace training toggle removed**: the workspace-level "use workspace content for model training" setting was removed, since Business and Enterprise plan data is already excluded from training by default

## What happened next

The WhatsApp and Power BI connectors extend Lovable's reach further into business operations and reporting workflows beyond its usual web-app and database use cases, while the GPT Image 2.5 rollout keeps Lovable's built-in image generation aligned with OpenAI's latest release. Combined with the skip-behavior fix, the update leans toward making Lovable's defaults less surprising for builders working across support, analytics, and design tasks in the same project. Full changelog entries are at docs.lovable.dev/changelog.
