Lovable's September 25, 2026 changelog headlines two new app connectors, Discord and Google Business Profile, alongside support for OpenAI's GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna models, a redesigned account settings dialog, and clickable citations for web sources.

## Details

- **Discord connector**: lets apps post messages to a Discord channel, list servers a bot has joined, read recent channel history, and respond to slash commands — useful for order and signup alerts, support handoffs from a contact form, or announcement boards
- **Google Business Profile connector**: lets apps read and update business profiles on Google Search and Maps, including opening hours, reviews and owner replies, local posts and photos, verification status, and performance metrics — useful for review responses, hours updates, and multi-location dashboards
- **GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna**: both new OpenAI models are now available for an app's own AI features, accepting text and image input and responding with text; users can ask Lovable to use a specific model or let it choose
- **Account settings dialog**: personal settings (profile, login methods, preferences, security, desktop/mobile app access) now open in a dedicated dialog from the sidebar avatar or via Cmd+. (Mac) / Ctrl+. (Windows/Linux), separate from workspace-level settings which stay on the workspace settings page
- **Clickable web source citations**: each web source Lovable cites in an answer, in both Chats and project chat, now appears as a small pill showing the site's icon and domain; hovering shows the page title and an excerpt, and clicking opens the source in a new tab

## What happened next

The release lands a day after the Storyblok connector's move to full content management, continuing Lovable's near-daily changelog cadence through late September. Adding Discord and Google Business Profile broadens the range of external services Lovable-built apps can act on directly, while GPT-6 Sol/Luna support gives developers another model choice for in-app AI features without needing separate API credentials.
