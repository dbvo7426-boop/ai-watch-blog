GitHub began gradually reopening signups for Copilot Business and Copilot Enterprise for customers paying by credit card or PayPal, rolling out over roughly two weeks starting September 3, 2026.

## Details

- **New billing model**: starting October 1, 2026, all seat assignments require upfront payment, applying to both new and existing customers on credit card or PayPal billing — other payment methods, such as invoice-based enterprise billing, are unaffected
- **Overage handling**: usage beyond included allowances incurs overage fees, and usage allowances may be prorated on a monthly basis
- **Scope of the reopening**: limited specifically to credit card and PayPal customers; the changelog doesn't detail why signups were paused, though the timing lines up with GitHub's broader billing overhaul announced in late August
- **Re-subscribing customers**: those who fully cancel and later resubscribe may go through updated vetting and be subject to the new billing terms

## What happened next

The reopening follows directly from GitHub's late-August announcement of policy and billing changes, putting the upfront-payment model into effect as signups resume — new Business and Enterprise customers joining now will do so under the October 1 billing terms from day one.
