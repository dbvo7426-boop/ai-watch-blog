xAI introduced Team Bots on September 28, 2026, a shared version of Grok Bot that lets an entire team work alongside the same AI coworker while each person's individual conversations stay private. The feature entered public beta on Teams and Enterprise plans.

## Details

- **What a Team Bot carries**: shared context (files, instructions, skills), plugins (integrations such as Salesforce, Notion, and GitHub), credentials (third-party API access), and memories that improve from the whole team's use over time
- **Privacy model**: expertise and skills accumulate at the team level, but each member's own chats and working context with the bot remain separate from everyone else's
- **Slack integration**: Team Bots get dedicated handles in Slack so a team can mention and collaborate with a bot the way they would a colleague
- **Example workflows xAI cites**: sales and customer-success bots that read company news, calls, and Slack threads to post morning account briefings (xAI says Harper Insurance cut customer recovery time to 24 hours, saving customers a cited $120,000); engineering bots that coordinate across Notion, Linear, Hex, Datadog, and Cursor to manage PR reviews, ticketing, and automated fixes; a marketing bot that checks drafts against brand guidelines and manages site changes through launch; and a data-analytics bot with read-only warehouse access that answers queries across more than 45,000 tables and remembers corrections
- **Availability**: public beta for Teams and Enterprise subscribers, with pre-built templates available to get started

## What happened next

Team Bots extends Grok Bot from a single-user "digital colleague" into a shared, persistent teammate that multiple people can rely on without losing their own private context. The launch follows xAI's steady widening of Grok Bot access over the summer (from a narrow SuperGrok Heavy/Cursor Ultra beta to broader subscription tiers) and its September push into creator incentives for bot templates, continuing a pattern of moving Grok Bot from novelty feature toward a core, monetizable part of xAI's product lineup.
