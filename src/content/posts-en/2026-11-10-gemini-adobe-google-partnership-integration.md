Google announced an expanded partnership with Adobe on November 10, 2026, at Adobe MAX.

## Integrations

- **Drive and Gmail to Acrobat:** a direct route from both services into Adobe Acrobat for PDF work. Acrobat appears as a suggested app, with an Edit with option in file previews. Google says it is coming soon.
- **Gemini and Gemini Enterprise:** Adobe is expanding its connectors to include Acrobat and additional creative tools.
- **Google Search:** Adobe creative tools will come to AI Mode.
- **Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker:** businesses can use this agent in Gemini Enterprise to help teams design graphics, work with PDFs and launch marketing campaigns.
- **Google DeepMind and Adobe:** the companies will explore new editing capabilities, starting with an image model for Photoshop that could enable near-real-time edits.

## Availability

Apart from the Drive and Gmail route being described as coming soon, launch dates and regions are undisclosed. The DeepMind work is exploratory with no committed timeline. Pricing and licensing are not mentioned.
