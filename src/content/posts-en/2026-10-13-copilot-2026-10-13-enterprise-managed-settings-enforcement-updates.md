GitHub added an enforcement update for Copilot's managed settings, letting enterprise administrators require supported clients to retrieve current server-managed settings rather than operating on stale local configuration.

## Details

- **What's new**: enterprise administrators can now require supported GitHub Copilot clients to retrieve current server-managed settings, rather than potentially operating on an outdated cached policy

## What happened next

This closes a gap in policy enforcement consistency across Copilot clients, landing the same week as the new client telemetry user-attribution controls as GitHub continues tightening enterprise governance options for Copilot deployments.
