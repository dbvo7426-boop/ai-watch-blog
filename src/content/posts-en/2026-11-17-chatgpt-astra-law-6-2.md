Astra for Law is a model configuration of GPT-6.2 Astra with an expanded legal search index and instructions for researching questions, weighing authorities and writing answers. The index has grown 34% since September and now exceeds 310 million URLs, including UK, Australian and Canadian sources.

- Harvey LAB-AA: ranked first, with full credit on 20.8% of 120 assignments, versus 15.0% for GPT-6.2 Astra and 13.1% for Muse Wonder Full. It used about 64,000 output tokens per assignment versus 139,000 for Muse Wonder Full.
- Hallucinations: 0.03 material hallucinations per assignment, tied with GPT-6 for lowest; Claude Opus 5.5 averaged 0.59.
- Vals AI Legal Research Bench: full credit on 112 of 208 questions (53.8%) in the November 16 evaluation, up from about 46% in September, earning 90.9% of available points.
- Availability: all ChatGPT Enterprise customers can request access through the Trusted Access Program in the admin console, then select `gpt-6.2-astra-law` in ChatGPT. API access is by request form.
