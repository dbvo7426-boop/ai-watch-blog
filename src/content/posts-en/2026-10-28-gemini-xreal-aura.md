Google and XREAL launched XREAL AURA, the first wired XR glasses powered by Android XR. Alongside the hardware, Google says Gemini gains a new ability starting today.

## Details

- **New Gemini feature:** share your view and Gemini can highlight what to focus on, in the room around you or on your screen. Gemini also works across everything in view, including a mirrored laptop window.
- **Hardware:** transparent lenses, 70-degree field of view, glasses under 95 grams, connected by cable to a compute puck with the battery and Qualcomm's new Snapdragon Reality Elite platform.
- **Features:** Wired Mirroring of a laptop over USB-C, window follow mode, spatial view that converts video, photo or games to 3D with higher-resolution support; Google Photos gets a spatial gallery later this year. Access to Google Play apps, including over 150 made for XR.
- **Availability and price:** orders open today for users 18+ in the U.S., Canada and Korea, and November 2 in Japan; launch pricing from USD 1,099 through the end of the year. U.S. shipments via Best Buy begin mid-November.

## What happened next

Most of the announcement is hardware and content, with the Gemini highlight feature as the one concrete AI addition. It extends Gemini's screen-aware assistance into spatial computing; how widely it reaches other Android XR devices is undisclosed.
