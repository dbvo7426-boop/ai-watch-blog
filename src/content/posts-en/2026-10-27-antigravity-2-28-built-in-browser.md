Antigravity App v2.28.0 was released on October 27, 2026, with 19 improvements and 15 fixes.

## Details

- **Built-in browser:** web pages open in the side panel.
- **Built-in PDF viewer:** PDFs can be viewed without leaving the app.
- **Attachments:** any file type can now be attached.
- **Diffs:** word and character highlighting.
- **Side panel:** a shortcut reopens closed side panel tabs.
- **Other items:** the individual list of improvements and fixes is not expanded in the changelog summary and is undisclosed here.

## What happened next

It continues the side panel work from 2.24 (redesign) and 2.27 (Markdown previews), turning the panel into a place to view web content and documents next to the agent conversation.
