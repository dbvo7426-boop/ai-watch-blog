OpenAI added iCloud Mail and Calendar plugins to ChatGPT on October 27, 2026.

## Details

- **What it does:** find emails, catch up on your inbox, check your schedule and update calendar events.
- **Availability:** all ChatGPT plans, on the web, the desktop app and iOS. Android availability is not mentioned.
- **How it works:** the plugins are connected apps; OpenAI points to its Connected apps in ChatGPT help article for how data is used.

## What happened next

This extends ChatGPT's connected-app coverage to Apple's mail and calendar services, which matters mostly for users outside Google and Microsoft ecosystems. Details on permissions and write-action confirmation are undisclosed in the release note.
