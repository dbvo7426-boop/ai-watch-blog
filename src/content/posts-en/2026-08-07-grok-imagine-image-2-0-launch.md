xAI released Imagine Image 2.0 on August 7, 2026, a new image generation and editing model built around a simple goal: producing images usable in real work, not just demos. It's now available as the new Quality Mode on grok.com/imagine and in the iOS and Android apps.

## Details

- **Precise instruction-following**: handles detailed prompts including typography and layout planning, multi-part visuals, and small text rendering that earlier models struggled with
- **Element preservation**: keeps specific elements consistent across repeated generations and edits, rather than redrawing the whole scene each time
- **Editing toolkit**: adds magic-wand selection for targeted region edits, segmentation for precise area selection, background removal with transparent export, and smart resize to recompose an image into a different aspect ratio
- **Multi-reference editing**: combines up to five input images in a single edit
- **Templates**: ships pre-configured workflows for photo editing, product shots, professional headshots, icons, game assets, and merchandise design
- **Cohesive world building**: can generate connected characters, locations, and props that maintain a consistent visual style across multiple images
- **Benchmark standing**: xAI cites Arena leaderboard rankings placing Imagine Image 2.0 second globally in both text-to-image generation and image editing as of the announcement date

## How to try it

- Live now as Quality Mode on grok.com/imagine, and in the Grok iOS and Android apps
- API access is "coming soon," per xAI; no pricing was disclosed in the announcement
