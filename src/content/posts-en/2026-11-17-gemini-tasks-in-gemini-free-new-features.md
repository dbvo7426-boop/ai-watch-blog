Google announced Tasks in the Gemini app on November 17, 2026. It is free in six countries: the U.S., Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia and India. It runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen, and some advanced features launch first in the U.S.

## How to use it

- **In the app:** flip the home-screen toggle from Chat to Task.
- **By text:** in Settings, tap Channels, then Set up under Text with Gemini and verify a phone number. This needs a U.S. number with an eligible carrier.
- **By email:** forward emails to gemini@google.com (starting in the U.S.).

## What it can do

- **Phone calls:** Gemini can call businesses for you, for example booking a service appointment and adding it to your calendar. Calling is not available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana or Nebraska.
- **Bills:** with Google Password Manager integration it can read a utility portal and past statements in email.
- **Shopping:** it compares products with Google's Shopping Graph, finds discount codes and checks out through Google Pay.
- **Groceries:** text items or a photo and Gemini hands the order off to Instacart.
- **Calendar:** forwarded school emails can become calendar events.
- **Connected apps:** it works with more than 60 apps, and developers can apply to the Connected Apps Platform.

## Payments

Purchases use the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2), with Mastercard and Visa supported and American Express coming soon. Cards use digital tokens, so points, cashback and purchase protections still apply. Users can set a hard spending cap. Receipts and tracking go to Google Wallet, which expands to iOS the same day. Support for the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) is upcoming.

## Security

- Tasks run in hardware-isolated virtual machines on Google Cloud's Agent Compute Engine, one dedicated VM per account.
- Prompt-injection filters and Google Safe Browsing limit which links Gemini opens.
- Gemini pauses for confirmation before critical steps such as clicking Buy. Routine actions can be set to Always allow.
- Users can change task settings, disconnect apps or delete chat history.

## Limitations

Google says some tasks fail or miss the mark. On sites that are still adapting, Gemini may finish most of a task and hand the screen back for the final click. Pricing beyond free availability is undisclosed.
