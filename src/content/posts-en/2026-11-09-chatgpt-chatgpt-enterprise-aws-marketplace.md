OpenAI listed ChatGPT Enterprise in AWS Marketplace. Organizations can discover, buy and manage it through their existing AWS purchasing and billing relationship, and apply eligible spend toward their AWS commitments.

## Details

- **Same product:** OpenAI says AWS customers get the same models, features and functionality as buying directly, with new features released at the same time. ChatGPT Enterprise covers ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, Codex and dots.
- **Also listed:** eligible customers can get ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Financial Services through AWS Marketplace.
- **Relation to Bedrock:** OpenAI describes this as complementing the OpenAI models in Amazon Bedrock, which developers use to build applications and agents.
- **How to start:** contact your OpenAI or AWS account team to confirm eligibility. Pricing was not disclosed.
