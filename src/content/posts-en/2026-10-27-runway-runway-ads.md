Runway added Runway Ads to its product on October 27, 2026. According to the changelog, the tool finds a user's next winning ad by analyzing their best-performing ads, producing new variants and publishing the approved ones.

## Details

- **Analysis of top performers:** Runway Ads reads a user's best-performing ads on Meta, Google and TikTok.
- **Variant generation:** It generates on-brand variants for every ad placement.
- **Publishing with approval:** Only the variants the user approves are published.
- **Availability:** Included with every paid plan; the changelog lists it under All Plans.
- **Not disclosed:** The changelog entry does not state pricing limits, the models used, or per-platform publishing requirements.

## What happened next

Runway Ads applies the company's generative video and image models to a concrete marketing workflow, moving beyond creative tools toward an end-to-end loop of measuring, generating and publishing. The human approval step before publishing suggests Runway is positioning it as an assisted workflow rather than fully autonomous ad buying. How well it performs against dedicated ad-tech tools is not yet known from the source.
