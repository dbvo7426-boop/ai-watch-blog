OpenAI has named Dali Rajic as Chief Revenue Officer, putting him in charge of the company's worldwide revenue organization as it scales its commercial operations.

## Details

- Rajic most recently served as President and COO at Wiz, the cybersecurity company acquired by Google, where he built out operational discipline during a period of rapid growth
- He previously held President and COO roles at Zscaler and served as Chief Customer and Revenue Officer at AppDynamics
- The appointment follows Denise Dresser's departure after she helped establish OpenAI's commercial framework during an earlier growth phase
- OpenAI says its products now serve more than 1 billion weekly active users and over 2 million businesses, with its business customer base doubling year-over-year
- OpenAI President and co-founder Greg Brockman said: "Dali will turn what we've learned into repeatable execution as we build out the full system"
- Rajic said: "OpenAI has extraordinary technology and enormous market pull. I'm excited to lead the revenue organization"

## What happened next
