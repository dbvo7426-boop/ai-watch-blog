OpenAI and the Government of Chile, led by the Ministry of Science, Technology, Knowledge and Innovation, signed a Memorandum of Understanding at Semafor's Silicon Valley & The World conference. It builds on Chile's National AI Policy and is supported by the U.S. Department of Commerce's American SI Exports Program.

- Public administration: exploring AI in administrative processes, including advice and training for ministry staff.
- Civil servants: exploring AI literacy and upskilling through workshops and a train-the-trainer approach.
- Startups: exploring access to OpenAI programs and technology, technical guidance and mentorship.
- A joint task force is expected to set priorities and an initial workplan. Specific activities, eligibility, resources and technology access will be agreed separately, and the MoU sets no investment or purchasing commitment.

OpenAI for Chile is part of OpenAI for Countries.
