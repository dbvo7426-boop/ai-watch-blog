Google launched Google Pics, an AI-powered image creation and editing tool for Google Workspace built on its Nano Banana image generation and editing model, letting users generate, refine, and collaborate on visuals without leaving their documents.

## Details

- **Core capabilities**: object segmentation to isolate and modify specific elements in an image while preserving the rest of the composition, in-image text editing and translation without disrupting the surrounding design, collaborative editing so teams can work on the same image together, and multiple design variations generated from a single text prompt
- **Workspace integration**: available now inside Google Docs and Slides, letting users create and edit images without switching to a separate app; Google Drive integration is coming in the following weeks
- **Availability**: rolling out over the coming weeks to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, as well as most Workspace business customers
- **Target use cases**: social media content, poster design, digital illustrations, and product mockups

## What happened next

Google Pics extends Nano Banana's image capabilities from a standalone generation tool into an embedded Workspace feature, following the same pattern as Google's other recent moves to fold generative AI directly into Docs, Sheets, and Slides rather than keeping it as a separate destination.
