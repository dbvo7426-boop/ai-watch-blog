Cursor announced on September 21, 2026 that Grok 4.7, xAI's newest model, is now available inside Cursor. xAI describes Grok 4.7 as its most capable model yet for long-running coding and knowledge work, and Cursor is one of the first coding tools to offer it alongside the Grok API, Grok Build, and other third-party platforms.

## Details

- **Built for extended tasks**: xAI says Grok 4.7 uses an expanded base architecture and additional reinforcement learning compared to Grok 4.6, aimed at self-verification over long, multi-step sessions
- **Coding benchmarks**: xAI reports 46.3% on CursorBench 4.0, 71.0% on DeepSWE v1.1, and 37.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0
- **Pricing**: $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, with a faster variant available at double the price for double the output speed
- **Safety note**: xAI cites HackerBench v0.3 results showing the model complies with only 3.3% of risky prompts
- **Availability**: ships in Cursor alongside existing model options, with no separate waitlist mentioned

## What happened next

Grok 4.7's launch triggered near-simultaneous rollout across coding tools; GitHub Copilot added the model the same week, and Cursor's addition means developers on either platform can pick it for agentic coding work without waiting on a broader release. Cursor gave no indication of retiring Grok 4.6 access, so both versions currently sit side by side in its model picker.
