xAI widened access to Grok Bot on August 21, 2026, just ten days after launching it in early beta exclusively for SuperGrok Heavy and Cursor Ultra/Teams Premium subscribers. The always-on AI teammate is now included with SuperGrok Plus, Cursor Pro+, Cursor Ultra, and both Standard and Premium Cursor Teams plans.

## Details

- **Wider tier access**: Grok Bot moves from a narrow, high-end-only beta to a lineup covering SuperGrok Plus, SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Pro+, Cursor Ultra, Cursor Teams Standard, and Cursor Teams Premium
- **Same core pitch**: xAI still describes each bot as "a highly capable digital colleague" that runs on its own cloud computer with browser and terminal access, working inside a user's actual tools rather than producing drafts for a human to execute
- **Multi-bot workflows**: users can run several bots at once through a chat-like interface, coordinating tasks across desktop and mobile
- **Use cases xAI highlights**: sales prospecting, website building, email management, customer support handling, and even meeting attendance
- **Enterprise still gated**: larger organizational rollouts remain on a waitlist rather than general availability

## What happened next

Standard subscribers on the newly added plans can start using Grok Bot immediately from their existing account; enterprise customers continue to queue on the waitlist xAI opened at launch. The expansion follows quickly on Grok 4.6's broader distribution push (Vertex AI, GitHub Copilot) earlier in August, underscoring how fast xAI is trying to move Grok Bot from novelty beta to a default part of its subscription plans.
