Anthropic, PBC launched the roadshow for its proposed initial public offering of Class A common stock on November 10, 2026.

## Offering terms

- **Total shares:** 825,000,000, of which Anthropic is offering 801,351,354 and certain existing stockholders are offering 23,648,646. Anthropic will not receive proceeds from the shares sold by stockholders.
- **Underwriter option:** a 30-day option to buy up to 123,750,000 additional shares.
- **Expected price range:** $70.00 to $78.00 per share.
- **Listing:** Anthropic has applied to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol ANT.
- **Prospectus sources:** Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC.

## Status

The registration statement has been filed with the U.S. SEC but is not yet effective. The announcement states it is not an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy the securities. The pricing date is undisclosed.
