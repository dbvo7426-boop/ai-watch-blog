Lovable has added a bundle of third-party AI models and tools that builders can wire directly into the apps they create, letting a single prompt add real-time voice, research, web data, or fast decision-making to an app without leaving the platform.

## Details

- **GPT-Live (OpenAI)**: real-time voice conversation for in-app assistants that answer questions, complete tasks, or teach — Lovable's own demo lets users talk to Rocky from *Project Hail Mary*; example use cases include an appointment-booking assistant, a language-exam speaking tutor, and a sales pitch coach
- **Claude models (Anthropic)**: access to the latest Claude models for reasoning, planning, and finished output, for apps such as a client-brief-to-proposal generator, a marketplace-photo value estimator, or an inbox assistant that flags urgent messages and drafts replies
- **Google models and tools**: Gemini Omni (video generation/animation from photos or other inputs), Lyria (original music generation with lyrics, style, tempo, and vocal choice), and Gemini 4 Argon (research and analysis) — covering apps like a product-photo-to-promo-video tool with soundtrack, a real-estate walkthrough clip generator, and a market-research tool that reads competitor reports and trend charts
- **Perplexity**: real-time, cited answers from the live web, for use cases like a morning briefing built from overnight news or a city guide with linked weekend events
- **Firecrawl**: search, scrape, and clean-data extraction from websites, for a competitor price tracker or a documentation-grounded help-center assistant
- **Parallel**: an AI web search platform for synthesizing reliable information, from quick lookups to long-range research, covering competitive intelligence, lead enrichment, research-paper monitoring, fundraise tracking, and grant/RFP finding
- **Jev by Typesafe AI**: fast categorical decisions — tags, scores, yes/no calls — that an app can act on immediately; Lovable gives an example pairing Jev with Claude so Jev sorts incoming requests while Claude drafts the reply
- **How it works**: builders describe the feature in chat (e.g. "Add a research panel that gives me a cited summary of my competitors"), and Lovable selects a suitable model or tool automatically, or the model/tool can be named directly, such as "use Opus 5.5" or "use Perplexity"

## What happened next

The release consolidates access to seven outside AI providers behind a single natural-language interface, continuing Lovable's push to make its builder a front end not just for app code but for a growing library of third-party intelligence. By letting builders either describe an outcome or name a specific model, Lovable keeps the model-agnostic positioning it has leaned on throughout 2026, while expanding what a prompt-built app can actually do once it ships.
