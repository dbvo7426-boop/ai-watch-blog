Lovable made its mobile app easier to find on September 2, 2026, adding a dedicated download section directly inside account settings.

## Details

- **Where it lives**: a new "On your phone" section now sits in Settings → Devices & apps, alongside the existing desktop app download options
- **How it works**: the section displays QR codes for both the iOS and Android apps, which users can scan with their phone to jump straight to the relevant app store listing
- **Alternative access**: standard app store buttons are also provided for anyone who prefers not to scan a code

## What happened next

It's a small convenience change rather than a new capability, but it removes friction for builders who want to check on projects or approve changes from their phone without searching an app store manually. The entry is documented in Lovable's official changelog at docs.lovable.dev/changelog.
