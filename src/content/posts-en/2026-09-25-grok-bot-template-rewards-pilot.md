X began an invite-only pilot on September 25, 2026 that pays creators for Grok Bot templates other users find useful, documenting the rules in a dedicated help center article and a companion legal terms page.

## Details

- **What it pays for**: creators earn discretionary rewards, in US dollars, for public Grok Bot templates that other people actually adopt and keep using
- **How rewards are set**: X recalculates payouts every two weeks, weighing how many people use a given template and whether they keep returning to it over time; a creator's own use of their own template does not count
- **Payout mechanism**: rewards land in a creator's X Money balance through X's regular creator payout run; X states amounts are discretionary, not wages, and nothing is owed unless X decides to pay it
- **Eligibility**: invite-only for now, restricted to users 18 or older in the United States with a Grok Bot account, an X account in good standing, a verified email, an active X Money balance, and an eligible X Premium or Premium Business subscription
- **Promotion requirement**: participants must also publicly post about their bots, unedited, at least twice per pay period
- **Timeline**: X describes the pilot as running for an anticipated two months from launch

## What happened next

The pilot builds directly on Grok Bot, the always-on AI agent xAI introduced in beta over the summer and has since been expanding across subscription tiers. By putting real money behind template creation, X is trying to seed a library of reusable, proven Grok Bot workflows faster than organic adoption alone would produce, while keeping the program small and invite-only until it sees how creators respond.
