Suno pushed a Studio update on September 17, 2026, focused on tightening up how the tool handles MIDI, alongside a smaller clip-management addition.

## Details

- **Better MIDI transcription**: Suno says transcription from MIDI to audio is now "faster, has more musical context, and is more accurate to the original"
- **Chat-driven stems**: users can ask Studio's chat bar to create new audio stems based on a MIDI clip and get more reliable results
- **Stronger MIDI-from-scratch prompting**: composing MIDI from a text prompt now produces more consistent output
- **Reinterpretation workflow**: users can upload an existing MIDI composition and have Studio reinterpret it in a different instrumental style — for example, turning a synth line into a guitar part or a gamelan arrangement
- **Clip renaming**: individual clips can be renamed via right-click, or renamed in bulk through a chat prompt such as "rename all my clips to reflect the track names"

## What happened next

The update is a production-tooling refinement rather than a model release, arriving about a week after Suno's v6 model family launched on September 9. It signals Suno continuing to build out Studio's DAW-like editing layer — MIDI import, transcription, and stem generation — in parallel with upgrades to the underlying generation models themselves.
