Google announced on November 23, 2026 that more apps can be connected to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode in Search. These join Gmail, Calendar and Photos.

- **Google Wallet:** starts rolling out today, as an opt-in connection in both AI Mode and the Gemini app. Google says it can help map financial milestones, find ways to trim everyday spending and maximize credit card benefits such as cash-back categories, travel perks and reward points. AI Mode can only read the information; it cannot move, manage or invest money.
- **Google Drive:** coming in the coming weeks. Responses can take context from docs, sheets and PDFs into account, for example a personalized preparation plan ahead of tax season.
- **Gemini app:** also coming in the coming weeks. Context can be shared between the Gemini app and AI Mode, so an idea explored in one carries over to the other.

**Controls:** open Search Personalization, select Connected Apps and choose the apps to connect. Users decide if and when to opt in and can turn connections off at any time. Google says users will soon be able to see the context AI Mode used through a "Personalization" icon under tailored responses, and can ask AI Mode to change it, such as saying they are no longer a vegetarian.

**Also:** AI Overviews and AI Mode will show more personally relevant responses based on recent search context, such as picking up a trip-planning session in a new thread. Personalization can be turned off in Search Settings.

Supported countries, languages and plan requirements were not disclosed in the post.
