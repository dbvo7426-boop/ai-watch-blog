Replit's updates page lists a Platform entry dated November 6, 2026, titled Get support directly from Agent.

## Details

- **Troubleshooting first:** Agent can help troubleshoot problems before you file a support ticket, all within your conversation.
- **What it checks:** When you ask about contacting Support, Agent checks for active incidents, reviews documentation, and looks into known issues or platform limitations.
- **Tickets:** Pro users can have Agent submit a support ticket for them. Agent includes debugging details from the environment, so you do not have to re-explain the problem and Support has more context.
- **Coverage:** Support availability and coverage are described in Replit's support policy.
