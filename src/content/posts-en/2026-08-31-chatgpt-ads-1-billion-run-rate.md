OpenAI announced that ChatGPT Ads has reached a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate in less than 200 days since launch, with tens of thousands of advertisers now using the platform and self-service access expanding into new regions.

## Details

- **Revenue milestone**: the $1 billion annualized run rate arrived in under 200 days, with advertisers outside the U.S. now representing a growing share of that revenue
- **Market expansion**: ChatGPT Ads is now available in over 40 countries, with self-service access newly launching in India, Europe, and the Middle East and North Africa; many of OpenAI's largest advertising clients now run campaigns across multiple markets at once
- **How ads work**: OpenAI says ads are "always clearly labeled and separate from ChatGPT's answers," with relevance driven by the current conversation and, where a country and user's settings allow it, broader ChatGPT history — while stressing that "advertising does not influence the answers ChatGPT provides" and that advertisers never see users' private conversations
- **Advertiser base has broadened**: the program started as a managed-sales, agency-led model and has since added more than 50 technology and measurement partners; small and medium-sized businesses now account for a material share of revenue since the self-service Ads Manager launched in May, with features like CPC bidding, outcome-optimized campaigns, product feeds, and custom audiences
- **Scale context**: OpenAI says ChatGPT now has over 1 billion weekly active users, and cited early advertiser results including a 3x return on ad spend over 28 days for one ecommerce client and 80%+ new-customer traffic for a technology partner

## What happened next

The milestone underscores how quickly OpenAI has built a full-fledged ad business on top of ChatGPT's user base, following up on the platform's earlier European rollout with a broader push into India and the Middle East/North Africa. With self-service tools now driving meaningful SMB revenue, OpenAI appears to be positioning ChatGPT Ads as a mainstream acquisition channel rather than a niche managed-sales product.
