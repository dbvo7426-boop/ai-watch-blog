Google announced Gemini computer use for Mac on November 19, 2026. You describe a repetitive multi-step workflow and Gemini carries it out across local files, desktop apps and the web, including clicking, typing, menu navigation and form entry.

## How it works

- It runs in an isolated background window with a live preview in the chat, so your main screen and cursor stay free.
- You choose which apps Gemini can access, can watch each step and pause at any time, and approve key actions before they happen.

## Example uses from Google

- Batch color-grading photos in design software.
- Pulling weights, dimensions and addresses from local PDFs to fill in shipping quotes.
- Collecting dashboard metrics and screenshots into a Google Slides deck.

## Availability and requirements

- Rolling out now in English in the U.S.; download at gemini.google/mac.
- Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS Sequoia 15.0 or later, a personal Google account for users 18 and older, and a Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra subscription.
