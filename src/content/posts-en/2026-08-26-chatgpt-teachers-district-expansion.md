OpenAI is expanding ChatGPT for Teachers to 55 additional U.S. school systems across 20 states, bringing its total footprint to more than 100 K-12 organizations in 30 states and free access for over 300,000 educators and staff — including roughly one in five of America's 20 largest public school districts.

## Details

- **Free through June 2028**: verified U.S. K-12 educators get ChatGPT for Teachers at no cost, paired with a dedicated K-12 Educator plugin for lesson planning, adapting materials, and building classroom resources
- **Training infrastructure**: OpenAI Academy training programs and "AI Skills Jams" (1,600+ teachers participated this summer) back the rollout, alongside a 5-year National Academy for AI Instruction partnership with the American Federation of Teachers targeting 400,000 educators
- **Privacy commitments**: a 16-state National Data Privacy Agreement through the Student Data Privacy Consortium underpins the program, and data in ChatGPT for Teachers workspaces is not used for model training by default
- **Usage so far**: from January to July 2026, educators sent 1.9 million messages — 900,000 related to report cards/progress reports, 800,000 for lesson planning, and 100,000+ each for substitute plans and evaluation materials
- **Reported outcomes**: OpenAI says 93% of Skills Jam participants left with resources they could apply directly, and 96% planned to use what they learned within 30 days

## What happened next

The district expansion continues OpenAI's push to make ChatGPT for Teachers a standard tool in U.S. public education rather than a pilot program, with the privacy agreement and educator-training infrastructure aimed at addressing the two biggest points of friction — data handling and teacher readiness — as adoption scales into more states.
