OpenAI Academy is running free, hands-on ChatGPT workshops for caregivers, timed to National Family Caregivers Month. The AI Skills Jam for Caregivers and Community Health is held in person with the Caregiver Action Network, the Alzheimer's Association and the American Diabetes Association.

## Details

- **Cities:** Oakland (California), Tampa (Florida), Pittsburgh (Pennsylvania), Atlanta (Georgia) and Detroit (Michigan).
- **Who it is for:** individual and family caregivers, caregiver support organizations, and people living with chronic or serious health conditions. No coding or technical background is required.
- **Topics:** organizing caregiver schedules and checklists, turning trusted educational resources into plain-language materials, finding community resources, and preparing discussion guides for support groups.
- **Responsible use:** participants learn to protect personal information, recognize the limits of AI tools and know when to consult healthcare professionals. AI should complement, not replace, medical, legal or financial advice.
- **Context:** OpenAI cites an internal analysis estimating that more than 40 million weekly ChatGPT messages about U.S. healthcare concern care for someone else. Participant input will shape future learning resources.
