As announced on September 18, GitHub deprecated six models across most Copilot experiences on October 19, 2026, including Copilot Chat, inline edits, ask and agent modes, and code completions.

## Details

- **Gemini 3.7 Flash:** suggested alternative Gemini 3.8 Flash.
- **GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.4:** suggested alternative GPT-6.1 Sol.
- **GPT-5.4 mini and GPT-5 mini:** suggested alternative GPT-6 Luna.
- **Grok 4.5:** suggested alternative Grok 4.7.
- **Admin action:** Copilot Enterprise administrators may need to enable alternatives through model policies; once enabled they appear in the model selector in VS Code and on github.com. No action is needed to remove the deprecated models.

## What happened next

Teams with pinned models in workflows or integrations should switch to the suggested replacements. The new model policy API, released the same day, can help administrators manage this at scale.
