OpenAI announced three updates to ChatGPT for Healthcare: computer-use agents that carry out multi-step work across existing healthcare software, improved trusted clinical search, and more than 20 new healthcare plugins. A companion post covers the computer-use results in detail.

## Computer-use agents

- The agents read screens, click, type and move between applications, so they can handle steps that APIs do not fully cover, such as EHRs and payer portals.
- OpenAI cites more capable, safer and cheaper models for clinical and administrative workflows, plus access controls and audit logs for secure deployments. Organizations choose which applications agents can access and which steps need human review.
- In a demo with synthetic patient data, an agent reviews visit notes, therapy records and prior imaging, drafts an MRI order, an encounter note and a patient reply for clinician review, then prepares a prior authorization request in the insurer's portal.
- Evaluation figures from the companion post: on EHR inbox tasks, 99% of 325 reviewed tasks were rated clinically safe, and physicians rated helpfulness as very or extremely helpful in 93% of 646 assessments. On HealthAdminBench (an independent Stanford benchmark), GPT-6 Astra earned 94.3% of rubric points and GPT-6 Luna 91.8% at 8.5 cents per task, both at maximum reasoning effort.
- Organizations named as working on deployments: Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare and CVS Health (for example referrals, document transfers, authorization requests and care-manager outreach).

## Clinical search and plugins

- Trusted clinical search in ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Clinicians now finds, evaluates and cites medical evidence better. In a blind evaluation of over 1,300 physician reviews, the updated experience was rated higher on all eight dimensions, including factual correctness, safety, actionability and source authoritativeness.
- More than 20 new plugins from companies such as Luma Health, TriZetto, Metriport, Redox, Oracle HDI, Availity Extend, UpToDate Expert AI and Turquoise are available in ChatGPT for Healthcare, including through ChartGPT in the EHR workflow. Uses include reviewing patient records, preparing prescriptions for clinician review, coordinating outreach, checking insurance eligibility and analyzing costs.

## Availability

- ChatGPT for Healthcare customers: computer-use agents (in ChatGPT Work and Codex) and improved clinical search are available now, subject to workspace settings. Administrators must enable the new plugins.
- Individual clinicians: free ChatGPT for Clinicians accounts are open to eligible, verified U.S. clinicians; computer use and improved clinical search are available now, along with select plugins.
- Developers: computer-use capability is available in the OpenAI API, ChatGPT Work and Codex; OpenAI directs interested parties to its sales team.
- A BAA with OpenAI is offered to support HIPAA-compliant workflows, and content shared with ChatGPT for Healthcare is not used to train models.
