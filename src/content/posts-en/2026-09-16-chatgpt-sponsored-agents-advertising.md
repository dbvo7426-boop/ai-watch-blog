OpenAI is testing a new conversational ad format called Sponsored Agents, alongside a set of new ad-creation tools and platform integrations for ChatGPT Ads.

## Details

- **Sponsored Agents**: users who click a business-sponsored ad can start a clearly-labeled conversation with that business's agent to ask clarifying questions before visiting its website, currently in limited testing with select U.S. advertisers
- **Ad creation tools**: marketers can build ads by writing natural-language prompts directly in ChatGPT Work, and Ads Manager now suggests AI-generated ad copy and imagery based on a campaign's landing page and objectives, which advertisers must review and approve before anything goes live
- **Automatic customization**: advertisers can enable automatic headline and description adaptation to fit the context of a given conversation, plus automatic translation into a user's preferred language
- **New platform integrations**: a HubSpot integration lets businesses create, track, and manage ChatGPT ads directly from their CRM, available now; a ChatGPT Ads app also launched in the U.S. Shopify App Store, with international rollout beginning September 23

## What happened next

Sponsored Agents pushes ChatGPT Ads beyond static ad units into interactive, business-specific conversations gated behind a click, following the platform's earlier milestone of a $1 billion annualized run rate — while the HubSpot and Shopify integrations extend ChatGPT Ads' reach directly into tools marketers and merchants already use for campaign management.
