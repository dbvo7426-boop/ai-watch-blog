OpenAI announced College Planner for ChatGPT for Teens, alongside upgraded study tools, aimed at helping high school students manage college applications and coursework.

## Details

- **College Planner**: consolidates application requirements, deadlines, tasks, and financial-aid steps for schools on a student's list into one plan, letting students track progress and ask ChatGPT clarifying questions about requirements and next steps
- **Initial scope**: launching for US students in grades 10-12 planning to attend four-year colleges, with expansion planned to other countries and institution types such as two-year colleges and trade schools
- **Financial aid support**: tools to understand aid requirements, track deadlines across schools, and explore scholarships and fee waivers
- **Flashcards**: students can upload notes or select topics to generate review cards; the system marks mastered cards, saves decks to a Library, and supports scheduled practice sessions
- **Quizzes**: an enhanced quiz tool converts uploaded notes into interactive assessments, with improved recognition of quiz requests within conversations
- **Multi-photo capture**: available now on iOS (Android in development), letting students photograph multiple note pages that automatically combine into a single PDF for upload
- **Safety oversight**: developed in partnership with Boston Children's Hospital's Digital Wellness Lab and an independent Student Advisory Council launching in 2026-27 to gather teen feedback on safety defaults, parental controls, notifications, and AI literacy

## What happened next

The release extends ChatGPT for Teens from a general-purpose assistant toward a dedicated academic-planning tool, continuing OpenAI's pattern of pairing teen-focused product expansion with outside safety oversight rather than shipping features unilaterally.
