Suno has redesigned the lyrics-writing experience on its web app, giving creators more control over their words before they ever hit generate. The update bundles four frequently requested capabilities into a single overhauled editor.

## Details

- **Lyricist**: A new reusable style profile that lets creators save examples of their own lyrics as a writing identity, so future songs can draw on a consistent voice and tone
- **Natural-language editing**: Creators can type a plain-language instruction — like "make this line funnier" — directly into the editor, and it revises the line in place instead of requiring a manual rewrite-and-paste loop
- **Rhyme and reference suggestions**: Highlighting a word or line surfaces rhyme options and alternate phrasings for inspiration
- **Song structure labels**: Sections can be tagged as Verse, Chorus, Outro, and similar, which tells the underlying model how the track should be structured
- **Full-screen writing mode**: A distraction-free, full-screen view with autosave, aimed at longer or more deliberate writing sessions
- **Scope of the change**: Suno is explicit that none of this changes how audio is generated — it is entirely about giving creators more control over lyrics before generation happens

## What happened next

- The redesigned editor is live now on Suno's web app; no separate opt-in was mentioned
- Suno published the update via its official release notes page (suno.com/release-notes), dated July 9, 2026
- Since then, Suno has continued shipping smaller web updates, including a song-duration slider (July 20, 2026) and iterative cover-art editing (July 31, 2026)
