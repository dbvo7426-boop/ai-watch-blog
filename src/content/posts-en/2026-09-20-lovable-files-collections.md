Lovable redesigned its project Files tab on September 20, 2026, adding collections so files can be grouped in more than one way at a time instead of sitting in a single flat list.

## Details

- **Overlapping collections**: files can now be grouped into collections, and a single file can belong to several collections at once rather than being filed under just one category
- **Upload and drag-and-drop**: users can upload their own files directly into the Files tab or drag them onto it
- **Files appear as they're generated**: new files created by Lovable while building an app show up in the tab automatically as they're produced
- **AI-assisted organization**: users can ask Lovable to organize files into collections automatically rather than sorting them by hand

## What happened next

The redesign targets projects that have accumulated a large mix of uploaded assets, generated code, and reference files, giving builders a way to keep them browsable without forcing a single folder hierarchy. It's the first of three changelog updates Lovable shipped in the same week, alongside a broader chat and workspace update on September 21 and a Parallel web-research connector on September 22.
