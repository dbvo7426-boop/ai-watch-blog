Google announced new Connected Apps on November 20, 2026, for the U.S. only.

- **Groceries:** Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw's and Target in Search and the Gemini app. Users can build an order and check out, powered by the Universal Commerce Protocol.
- **Experian:** already live in the Gemini app and now rolling out to Search, for checking a credit rating and shopping for a personal loan.
- **H&R Block:** available in Search for calculating taxes and estimating refunds.
- **Angi and Thumbtack:** coming to Search in the coming weeks for finding local home-service pros. No firm date was given.

Pricing, fees and data-handling details were not disclosed in the post.
