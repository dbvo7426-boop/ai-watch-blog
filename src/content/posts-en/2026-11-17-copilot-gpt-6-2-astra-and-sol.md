OpenAI GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol are generally available in GitHub Copilot as of November 17, 2026, with a gradual rollout.

- **GPT-6.2 Astra**: aimed at agentic coding and terminal work.
- **GPT-6.2 Sol**: aimed at complex agentic coding and in-depth code review. Per the announcement, early testing showed it catching nearly twice as many real issues as GPT-6.1 Sol.

**Plans:** Copilot Pro+, Max, Business and Enterprise.

**Where:** the model picker in Visual Studio Code, Visual Studio, Copilot CLI, the Copilot coding agent, the Copilot app, github.com, GitHub Mobile (iOS and Android), JetBrains IDEs, Xcode and Eclipse.

**Billing and admin:** usage-based billing at provider list pricing; no multipliers are listed. Business and Enterprise admins manage access through the model policy in Copilot settings, and default model enablement turns new models on unless the global default or the model was disabled.
