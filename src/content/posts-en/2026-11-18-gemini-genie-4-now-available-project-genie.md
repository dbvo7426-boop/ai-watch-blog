Google announced on November 18, 2026 that Genie 4 is rolling out to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers through Project Genie (labs.google/projectgenie). Google AI Pro members can join a waitlist. This follows the October 27 research announcement, which said Ultra availability was coming soon.

## Genie 4

- Type new prompts mid-session to edit the world in real time, such as changing the weather, adding a character or redirecting the action.
- Real-time native audio adapts to the scene, from ambient soundscapes to character dialogue.

## Genie 3 updates (still in the model picker)

- Runs extend to up to 3 minutes per generation, up from 60 seconds.
- Multiple worlds can be linked for longer journeys.
- Worlds can be seeded from 360-degree panorama images.
- In-world events can be triggered with natural-language prompts.

## Project Genie agent and gallery

- The agent builds worlds from conversation; within a session Gemini can observe the screen and offer suggestion chips.
- A gallery holds curated and community worlds, and users can publish and remix worlds.

## Safety and limits

All generated video and audio carries SynthID watermarking, and inputs and outputs are safety-screened. Google lists camera latency, intricate physical interactions, real-time audio precision and long-term scene permanence as ongoing challenges. Supported countries, pricing and a Genie 4 session length limit are undisclosed.
