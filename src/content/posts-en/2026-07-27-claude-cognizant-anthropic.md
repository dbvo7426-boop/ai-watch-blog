On July 27, 2026, Anthropic announced an expanded partnership with Cognizant, a major IT services company. More than 30,000 Cognizant associates have now completed Claude training, and the company has embedded Claude into solutions for manufacturing, life sciences, insurance, and other industries. In recognition of this work, Cognizant has been named a Global Premier Partner — the top tier of Anthropic's Claude Partner Network.

## Details

- **Partnership scope**: Expanded integration of Claude across Cognizant's operations for clients in manufacturing, life sciences, insurance, and related sectors
- **Training milestone**: Over 30,000 Cognizant associates have completed Claude training
- **Partner tier**: Cognizant achieved Global Premier Partner status within Anthropic's Claude Partner Network
- **Platforms involved**: Claude is embedded across Cognizant's Flowsource™ (a full-stack engineering platform with Claude Code integration), Neuro® AI Engineering, and Neuro® IT Ops
- **Flowsource details**: Claude Code now operates within Flowsource's Spec-Driven Development module, using project specifications and architectural blueprints to generate and evaluate code
- **Client results**: A customer experience portal for a global manufacturer was completed within six months; a contract-intelligence system for a biopharmaceutical client cut contract review time by up to 40% with 88%+ extraction accuracy; a risk-navigation tool let insurance underwriters evaluate accounts in minutes instead of hours, saving roughly eight hours per person weekly
- **Executive comments**: Cognizant CEO Ravi Kumar S said, "AI capability is rising faster than enterprises can absorb it, and that gap is the defining problem of this moment." Anthropic co-founder Daniela Amodei said, "Deepening our partnership with Cognizant will help more companies harness AI's growing capability."

## How to try it

- This announcement concerns Cognizant's enterprise clients and partners, not a service available directly to individual users
- Existing Cognizant clients can contact their account teams to discuss adopting Claude within Flowsource or the Neuro platforms
- More on Anthropic's Claude Partner Network is available on Anthropic's official site
