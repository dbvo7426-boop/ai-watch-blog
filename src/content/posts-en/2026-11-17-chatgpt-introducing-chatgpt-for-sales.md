OpenAI announced ChatGPT for Sales: a Sales plugin with dedicated skills for sellers, plus more than 30 new and enhanced partner plugins. The Sales plugin is installed from chatgpt.com/plugins/sales.

Partner plugins by workflow:
- Content and enablement: Confluence, Guru, Seismic, Gamma, Figma, Canva, plus ChatGPT Voice, Slides and Sites.
- Accounts and deals: Salesforce, HubSpot, Close CRM, monday CRM.
- Prospecting: Apollo, Clay, Common Room, ZoomInfo, Outreach or Salesloft.
- Customer conversations: the Meetings plugin, Gong, Otter.ai, Granola.
- Performance and risk: Backstory, the Data plugin or Hex.
- Quotes and agreements: Docusign, Ironclad, PandaDoc, ServiceNow CPQ.

OpenAI describes dot as an always-on agent in ChatGPT that can use these plugins to prepare meeting briefs, track deal updates and propose next steps for review.

Availability of partner plugins varies by plan, region and workspace settings; which plans include which plugins is undisclosed. ChatGPT Business and Enterprise admins can enable plugins in the Admin Console, making them installable by members or installed by default.
