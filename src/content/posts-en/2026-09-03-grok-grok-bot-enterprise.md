xAI made Grok Bot available for enterprises on September 3, 2026, less than a month after the always-on AI agent launched in beta on August 11. Existing Grok and Cursor Enterprise customers get free access for two weeks and can invite their entire organization, including people who don't already have a seat.

## Details

- **Enterprise controls**: the release adds access, network, and audit controls so administrators can manage bots at organizational scale rather than per individual user
- **Isolated execution**: each user's Grok Bot runs in its own secure, isolated environment separate from every other user, with no default access — a bot only reaches the accounts it's explicitly signed into
- **Workflow templates**: users teach bots a workflow by demonstration, and those workflows can then be shared as templates across a team
- **Adoption so far**: xAI says thousands of organizations have adopted Grok Bot since its beta launch, including Legora, Supermicro, and ServiceTitan, with the heaviest usage outside of engineering teams
- **Use cases xAI highlights**: sales bots that watch webinars and draft outreach, recruiting bots that prospect overnight and build candidate shortlists, marketing bots that process webinar data and alert account executives, finance bots that monitor vendor spend, and engineering bots that watch PRs and run overnight code audits
- **Trial mechanics**: free two-week access activates through an admin dashboard, or by contacting xAI sales directly; no long-term enterprise pricing was disclosed

## What happened next

The enterprise launch follows a rapid expansion arc for Grok Bot: a narrow beta for SuperGrok Heavy and Cursor Ultra subscribers on August 11, broader tier access on August 21, integration with X on August 29, and now full enterprise rollout on September 3. xAI is positioning Grok Bot as a persistent digital workforce rather than a chat feature, competing directly with similar always-on agent products from OpenAI and Anthropic for enterprise workflow automation budgets.
