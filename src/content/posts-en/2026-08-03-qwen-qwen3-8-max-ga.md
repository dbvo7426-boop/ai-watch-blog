On August 3, 2026, Alibaba made "Qwen3.8-Max" widely accessible through its Model Studio APIs, dropping the "preview" label the 2.4-trillion-parameter flagship had carried since its July 19 unveiling. Alibaba says an open-weight release will follow "the following week," marking a return to open-sourcing its top-tier models after keeping several recent flagships closed.

## Details

- **Status change**: The model, previously offered only as `qwen3.8-max-preview`, is now listed in Alibaba's official model changelog simply as `qwen3.8-max`, Qwen's "most capable flagship model to date"
- **Specs**: 2.4 trillion parameters, Mixture-of-Experts architecture, hybrid thinking mode enabled by default, and a context window of up to 1 million tokens
- **Availability**: Accessible globally via Alibaba Cloud's Model Studio APIs, as well as through web and desktop apps
- **QwenWork public beta**: The same day, Alibaba opened a public beta for "QwenWork," a workplace AI agent platform built on the model
- **Capabilities highlighted**: Processing multiple data formats, recreating software from screenshots, generating interactive games and educational animations, and converting 2D floor plans into 3D visualizations
- **Open weights**: Alibaba says an open-weight release is planned for the following week, which — if it holds — would end the "closed-flagship" approach the company has followed since the Qwen3.7 generation
- **No pricing or third-party benchmarks** have been published yet

## What happened next

- Alibaba has not yet published per-token pricing or independent benchmark comparisons for the GA version
- The promised open-weight release, expected roughly a week out, is the next milestone to watch
