GitHub shared three updates on Copilot Fusion on November 17, 2026. With Fusion you pick it in the model picker, set an effort level, and Fusion chooses and coordinates models for each task.

- **Benchmarks:** Copilot Fusion is listed on the public Terminal-Bench 4.1 leaderboards hosted by Stanford, Harbor and the Laude Institute. Fusion Max reached a 71.2% resolution rate, 5.4 percentage points ahead of Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2, at 88% lower cost than Fable 5.2 and 57% lower than Opus 5.5.
- **Availability:** Fusion can be selected on the latest versions of github.com and Visual Studio, and in the GitHub Mobile beta apps for iOS and Android. Production GitHub Mobile support is coming shortly.
- **Models added:** Claude Sonnet 5.5 to Fusion Max, GPT-6.1 Terra to Fusion High, and MAI-Code-2 to Fusion Standard.

Plan tiers and pricing beyond the relative cost figures are undisclosed.
