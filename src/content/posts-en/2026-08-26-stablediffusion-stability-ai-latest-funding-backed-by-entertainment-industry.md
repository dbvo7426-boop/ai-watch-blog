Stability AI has closed a $76 million Series B round, bringing its total funding to $232 million, with an unusually deep bench of entertainment-industry companies and individuals joining as backers.

## Details

- **Round size**: $76 million Series B
- **Total funding to date**: $232 million (across equity rounds and convertible notes)
- **Valuation**: Not disclosed
- **Corporate investors from entertainment and media**:
  - Electronic Arts
  - Sony Music Group
  - Universal Music Group
  - Warner Music Group
  - WPP
- **Individual investors**: Sean Parker, Eric Schmidt, James Cameron, Kevin Mayer, Mark Burnett, Patrick Whitesell, Prem Akkaraju, Reuben Brothers, Robert Nelsen, and Vivi Nevo
- **Repeat backers**: Coatue, Greycroft, Kadmos Capital, Sean Parker, and Eric Schmidt returned for this round
- **New board member**: Thomas Laffont, co-founder of Coatue, is joining Stability AI's board of directors
- **Leadership**: The round comes under CEO Prem Akkaraju, the former Weta Digital chief who took the helm at Stability AI in June 2024
- **Use of funds**: Building out a creative production product suite, expanding applied research, and growing professional services
- **Quote**: "This unmatched group of investors affirms our vision where generative AI empowers every producer, musician, and storyteller," said CEO Prem Akkaraju

## What happened next

The involvement of major music labels (Sony, Universal, Warner) and a Hollywood-adjacent investor roster including James Cameron signals that entertainment incumbents are betting on Stability AI's generative tools for professional production pipelines rather than treating generative AI purely as a threat to creative industries. Expect Stability AI to lean further into enterprise creative-production tooling and partnerships with studios and labels following this raise.
