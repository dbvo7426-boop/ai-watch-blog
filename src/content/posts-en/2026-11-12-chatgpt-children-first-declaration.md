OpenAI said it signed the Charter of Engagement for Safer AI for Children at the Paris Peace Forum. The post says this happened earlier this week and gives no exact date.

## Commitments

- **Funding:** $2 million to a scientific research network under the Coalition for Children's Rights and Protection in the Age of AI. The split of the money and the grantees are undisclosed.
- **Purpose:** shared measurement work, including tools researchers can use to study AI's risks and benefits for children, to inform safer products and better policy.
- **Charter:** sets shared principles for safe, age-appropriate AI, a research program, and safeguards for research independence.
- **Independence:** research teams keep control of their conclusions, and all findings will be published, including negative or inconclusive results.

## Policy position

OpenAI supports enforceable, comprehensive youth AI safety laws that still let young people use AI for learning and creativity. It says such laws should include age-appropriate safeguards on by default, tools for parents, regular risk assessments and mitigation, and meaningful accountability. It plans to work with European policymakers as the EU Kids Act develops. The post announces no product changes.
