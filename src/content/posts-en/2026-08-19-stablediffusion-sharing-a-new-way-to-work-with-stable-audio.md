Stability AI has introduced two new ways to work with Stable Audio 3.0, shifting the tool from one-shot generation toward an iterative, in-workflow creative process: a plugin that brings generation directly into digital audio workstations, and an upgraded web experience at StableAudio.com.

## Details

### DAW plugin

- Generates audio directly on a track inside your DAW, where it can be arranged, edited, processed, and built on like any other track
- Supports tempo synchronization so generated audio locks to your project's BPM
- Generation length is customizable up to six minutes
- Multiple takes can be saved and compared before committing
- Ships as macOS AU and VST3 plugins, compatible with Logic Pro and Ableton Live

### Advanced web experience

- Directional prompts let you steer and refine a generation iteratively rather than starting over
- Audio-to-audio exploration generates genre and mood variations from an existing clip
- Individual track mixing controls (level, pan, mute) for multi-track outputs
- Per-track effects processing

Both tools run on Stable Audio 3.0, whose commercially-safe models give users full ownership and distribution rights over what they generate.

Stability AI describes this release as an early beta, and features are expected to keep evolving based on feedback.

## How to try it

Both the DAW plugin and the advanced web experience are available now at [StableAudio.com](https://stableaudio.com).
