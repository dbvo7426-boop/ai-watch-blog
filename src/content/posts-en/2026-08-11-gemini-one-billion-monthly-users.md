Google announced that the Gemini app has crossed 1 billion monthly active users, describing it as the fastest-growing product in the company's history and outlining the usage patterns behind that growth.

## Details

- **The milestone**: More than 1 billion people now use the Gemini app every month, a scale Google says it reached faster than any previous Google product
- **Voice-first usage**: 63% of users now talk directly to Gemini rather than typing, with a growing share of "voice only" users; busy parents are 43% more likely than average to rely on voice for everyday tasks
- **Gemini Live goes beyond voice**: One in five Gemini Live interactions now involve more than audio, with people sharing live camera feeds or their screen for real-time help
- **Education use**: 38% of school-related requests to Gemini now include an attachment, such as a photo of homework or a document
- **Scale of generation**: Google says the app now generates more than 150 million images per day, with image, video, and audio generation consolidated into a single product surface
- **Automation and platforms**: Gemini now supports automation across more than 40 popular apps for tasks like booking rides or restaurant reservations, and Google notes Gemini has more than 100 million active iOS users, with macOS users prompting roughly twice as often as users on other platforms

## What happened next

Google frames the milestone as a waypoint rather than an endpoint, stating its goal is to make Gemini "the most personal, proactive, and powerful assistant" while scaling for its next billion users. The announcement doubles as a broader positioning statement for Gemini against rival consumer AI assistants, leaning heavily on voice, live multimodal interaction, and cross-app automation as its primary growth drivers rather than raw model benchmarks.
