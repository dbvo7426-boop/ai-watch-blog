OpenAI rolled out support for connecting multiple Google accounts to ChatGPT on August 28, 2026, removing a long-standing limitation where each Google connector could only carry a single identity. Users can now link separate Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Contacts accounts side by side, so ChatGPT can pull from personal and work profiles within one conversation.

## Details

- **Multiple accounts per service**: users can connect more than one Gmail, Google Calendar, or Google Contacts account through the Plugins section in the sidebar
- **Adding an account**: click the gear icon on an already-connected plugin to add another Google identity for that same service
- **Cross-account requests**: ChatGPT can now, for example, "check your calendars together when planning your day" or "find emails across your connected inboxes" spanning multiple accounts
- **Availability**: rolled out across Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans, on web, desktop, iOS, and Android
- **Codex included**: the same multi-account support extends to Codex, OpenAI's coding agent, according to reporting on the rollout

## What happened next

The update responds to a frequently requested feature from users juggling personal and work Google accounts, a gap that had previously pushed some to third-party connector tools. It brings ChatGPT roughly in line with user expectations for calendar and email assistants that need to reason across more than one inbox, though OpenAI has not said whether the same multi-account support will extend to other connector types like Google Drive.
