Google announced new Gemini app features aimed at small business owners, rolling out globally in the coming days to all Gemini app users.

## Details

- **Business landing page:** a hub for building a brand, analyzing finances and reviewing customer insights.
- **Google integrations:** Gemini can connect to Google Business Profile, Google Ads and Google Workspace.
- **Third-party connections:** Adobe, Canva, Dropbox, Shopify, Wix, Zoho and Xero.
- **Brand style setup:** upload a website, brand kit, fonts or color palette for consistent visuals; Nano Banana 2.1 generates on-brand assets for launches and campaigns.
- **Financial insights:** turn spreadsheets, receipts and purchase records into graphics and interactive models.
- **New skills:** marketing and finance skills. Larger businesses are pointed to the separate Gemini for your business offering.

## What happened next

The update positions the consumer Gemini app as a lightweight business assistant. Exact rollout timing per region and plan limits were not disclosed.
