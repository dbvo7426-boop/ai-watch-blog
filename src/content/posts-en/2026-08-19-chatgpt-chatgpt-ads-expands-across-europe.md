OpenAI is expanding ChatGPT Ads to 31 European countries starting the following week, marking its largest expansion since the advertising product's U.S. pilot launch in February. The named markets include Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, the Netherlands, and Austria.

## Details

- **Scope**: 31 European markets in total, with Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, the Netherlands, and Austria specifically named
- **Who sees ads**: Ads appear only for users on the Free and Go subscription tiers; Plus, Pro, and Enterprise plans remain ad-free
- **Access rollout**: Initial access goes to OpenAI's own Ads Solutions team along with agency and technology partners; self-service access through Ads Manager is planned for later in the summer
- **Ad format and placement**: OpenAI says ads are "always clearly labeled and separate from ChatGPT's answers," and states that advertising does not influence the answers ChatGPT provides
- **Targeting and measurement**: Advertisers get geo-targeting and custom audience tools, conversion-optimization bidding beyond standard CPM/CPC models, and measurement through an OpenAI Pixel, a Conversions API, and third-party integrations
- **Privacy stance**: OpenAI says it keeps conversations private from advertisers and does not sell customer data, and that users retain control over ad personalization preferences (with ad-free paid tiers as an alternative)
- **Timeline context**: ChatGPT Ads launched as a U.S. pilot in February, expanded to eight more markets over the following six months, and this European rollout is described as the largest expansion to date

## What happened next

The European expansion is a straightforward scaling of a monetization product OpenAI has been testing since February, rather than a new format or capability. The bigger open questions — how ad load and personalization data handling hold up under EU privacy regulation, and how self-service advertiser access performs once it opens later in the summer — will shape how far OpenAI pushes ChatGPT Ads beyond these 31 markets.
