Anthropic announced an expansion of its scientific-research programs on August 27, 2026, opening 10,000 free Claude Team seats globally to principal investigators at academic and nonprofit institutions and broadening its AI for Science program beyond biology into fields like number theory and protein design.

## Details

- **Free Team seats**: 10,000 standard Claude Team seats are opening worldwide at no cost for one year, with premium seats offering 5x usage limits available for $15/month; Anthropic says it plans to add more seats beyond the initial batch
- **Wider AI for Science scope**: the program, previously centered on biological sciences, now covers other computationally heavy research areas, including work on the Riemann zeta function and protein design
- **Credits available**: qualifying researchers can apply for up to $50,000 in compute credits per project
- **Eligibility**: principal investigators or equivalents at academic or nonprofit institutions can register through a verification form
- **Claude Science context**: builds on Claude Science, the research-oriented product Anthropic launched in June that integrates common research software and produces auditable outputs
- **Access limits remain**: biology and chemistry researchers are currently restricted to Opus-class models, and Claude still declines certain professional biology/drug-development queries over dual-use concerns; Anthropic says it is working with the U.S. government on separate access programs for life-sciences professionals

## What happened next

Anthropic frames the expansion as part of a broader push to make Claude a standard research tool rather than a general-purpose add-on, following the same-day preview of its Model Hardware Standard for lab automation. Researchers can apply now through the verification form, though the practical impact will depend on how quickly Anthropic scales past the initial 10,000-seat cap and loosens model access for sensitive scientific domains.
