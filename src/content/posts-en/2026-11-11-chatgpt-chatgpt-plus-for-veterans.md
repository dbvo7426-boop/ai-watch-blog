OpenAI reported that more than 20,000 transitioning servicemembers and veterans have claimed a year of ChatGPT Plus at no cost over the past year. The effort began with veterans working at OpenAI, and OpenAI thanks ID.me for helping people access the offer. Detailed eligibility criteria and what happens after the free year are undisclosed.

## Training and events

- OpenAI Academy and community partners have held more than a dozen trainings for nearly 1,000 military community members, with seven more scheduled before year-end.
- A six-event Academy series with Veterans Forge has concluded.
- A workshop in Reston, Virginia drew nearly 100 veterans and active-duty servicemembers, and more than 80 Legionnaires attended three AI sessions at the American Legion's national convention in Louisville, Kentucky.
- Pilot workshops also ran with AI Collective Hampton Roads and NPower.
- Free self-directed resources include a veterans collection in OpenAI Academy and a set of 100 example chats for veterans.

OpenAI says it will continue the work with partners through the rest of the year and that the effort is shaping future veteran offerings.
