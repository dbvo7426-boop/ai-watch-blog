GitHub Copilot's content exclusion policies reached general availability in the Copilot app and Copilot CLI, letting administrators keep specific files out of Copilot's context across agentic workflows.

## Details

- **What it does**: files marked for exclusion are never used as context by Copilot, protecting sensitive or proprietary code from being read during suggestions or agent tasks
- **Who can configure it**: enterprise, organization, and repository administrators, using the same content exclusion policies already available on other Copilot surfaces
- **Where it now applies**: the Copilot app and Copilot CLI, extending protections that previously covered other surfaces
- **Availability**: Copilot Business and Copilot Enterprise customers

## What happened next

The GA rollout closes a gap where content exclusion rules configured elsewhere didn't consistently follow users into the Copilot app or CLI, giving administrators one consistent policy that applies everywhere Copilot runs.
