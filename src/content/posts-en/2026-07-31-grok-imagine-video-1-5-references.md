xAI announced that its video generation model "Grok Imagine Video 1.5" now supports image and voice references along with native 1080p output. It also gained text-only video generation, and can keep a character's voice consistent across multiple scenes.

## Details

- **Image and voice references**: Up to seven references (images and/or voice clips) per generation, letting a character's appearance and voice stay consistent across new scenes
- **Text-only video generation**: New capability to generate video from a text prompt alone, with no starting image required — the pipeline first generates an image, then animates it
- **Native 1080p**: Both text-to-video and image-to-video now support native 1080p output
- **Voice consistency**: Voice references let a character's voice stay consistent across multiple scenes
- **API access**: Available via the xAI API as `grok-imagine-video-1.5` (voice references available on request)

## How to try it

- Image and voice references launched first in the US for SuperGrok Heavy and SuperGrok Plus subscribers on grok.com/imagine and the iOS app, rolling out to all tiers over the next few days
- Text-only video generation and native 1080p output are already generally available across web, iOS, and Android
- Developers can access the `grok-imagine-video-1.5` model through the xAI API
