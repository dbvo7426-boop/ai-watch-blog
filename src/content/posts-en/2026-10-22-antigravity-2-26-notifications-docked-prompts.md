Google Antigravity shipped two app releases this week, v2.25.0 on October 20 and v2.26.0 on October 22. Both are mostly usability and reliability updates.

## Details

- **v2.26.0 (October 22)**: adds a Notifications page in Settings with sound and volume controls. Question and permission prompts now dock above the message box so users can keep typing while a prompt is open. The conversations palette gains sort and filter options. The release lists 8 improvements and 15 fixes.
- **v2.25.0 (October 20)**: adds a plugin badge on sidebar project folders managed by UI plugins, hides empty sidecar-managed projects, and shows the Google Cloud project auto-assignment panel during onboarding when no pre-assigned licenses are found. It includes 8 fixes, such as a macOS dock issue after closing the last window and a corrupted summaries database that broke conversation search.

## What happened next

Neither release adds a headline capability. They show Antigravity iterating on day-to-day agent supervision: docked prompts and notification controls make it easier to work while agents wait for input. No further releases were listed through October 24.
