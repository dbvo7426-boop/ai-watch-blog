Midjourney announced its first-ever corporate acquisition on July 23, 2026, buying astrology app Co-Star, which has more than 4 million monthly active users. Co-Star founder and CEO Banu Guler joins Midjourney as Chief Design Officer, leading a cross-functional team spanning product, design, and frontend.

## Details

- **The acquisition**: Midjourney acquired Co-Star and brought on founder Banu Guler as Chief Design Officer. Co-Star's roughly two-dozen-person team also joins Midjourney
- **Co-Star keeps operating independently**: The Co-Star app itself will continue to run independently under Guler's complete control after the deal
- **Founder David Holz's comment**: Holz said he respects Guler's insights immensely and is "excited to have an opportunity to have her and her amazing team join Midjourney to help build a broader organization around Product and Design"
- **What's next**: The acquisition is part of Midjourney's push into new research areas, starting with a new initiative called "Midjourney Medical," with plans to announce roughly half a dozen similar new initiatives over the next six months
- **Timing**: Reports indicate the deal itself closed in spring 2026, with the announcement only made public via the official blog post and Midjourney's X account on July 23
