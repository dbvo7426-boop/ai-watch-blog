Google rolled out a new wave of Connected Apps for Gemini, integrating third-party services across productivity, creative work, and everyday lifestyle tasks.

## Details

- **Productivity tools**: Airtable, Linear, monday.com, PandaDoc, Wispr AI, and Zoho, for managing projects, organizing databases, and capturing notes without leaving Gemini
- **Creative apps**: Adobe, Picsart, Squarespace, and Webflow, for designing visual assets and building websites directly within the platform
- **Lifestyle services**: apartments.com for housing searches, Experian for credit monitoring, Peloton for workout planning, and SeatGeek for event ticketing
- **How to use them**: activate integrations through Gemini settings, or invoke a specific app directly in a conversation with an @ mention
- **Rollout**: began September 23, 2026, with a phased deployment rather than immediate universal availability

## What happened next

The expansion pushes Gemini further toward being a single interface for tasks that would otherwise require switching between separate apps, spanning work tools, creative software, and personal errands in one rollout.
