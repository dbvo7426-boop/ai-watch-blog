Kiro released Kiro Mobile for Android on November 12, 2026. The app is on Google Play and requires a Pro, Pro+, Pro Max or Power plan.

## Details

- **Sign-in:** Sign in with your Kiro account. Sessions, connected repositories and model choices carry over, and your laptop does not need to stay on.
- **Sessions:** Start a cloud session, or continue one started in Kiro Web, the IDE or the CLI. You choose repositories and a model, and can turn on Build with spec to plan first.
- **While running:** Follow replies and tool activity live, attach images, answer the agent's questions, or stop a run.
- **Review:** Each edited file appears as a phone-sized diff card, and each session shows its pull request status. Review and merge still happen in your Git provider.

## Same day

CLI 3.3.0 added `/copy N` and `/copy last N`, a configurable context usage threshold for automatic compaction, and sub-agent tasks in the agent monitor.
