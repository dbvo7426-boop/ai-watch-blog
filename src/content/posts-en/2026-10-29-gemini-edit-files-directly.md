Google announced that the Gemini app can now generate, preview and edit documents, PDFs, presentations, spreadsheets, code and other files inside the chat window.

## Details

- **Editing:** Expand a file to full screen and type changes directly.
- **Highlight and ask:** Select a section and ask Gemini questions about it or request AI revisions, including text and visuals.
- **Example:** Upload handwritten lecture notes, have Gemini produce a PDF study guide, then edit it by hand and refine with AI.
- **File types:** PDF preview and editing works on web and mobile. Google Docs, Google Sheets, slide decks, Markdown, TXT and code are web only.
- **Availability:** All users, in all languages where the Gemini app is offered. Subscription tiers are not specified, and no phased rollout is described.

## What happened next

This moves Gemini from producing files for download to a working editor in the conversation, though mobile support is limited to PDFs. Any plan-based limits are undisclosed.
