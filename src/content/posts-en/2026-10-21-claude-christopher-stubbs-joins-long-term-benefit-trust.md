Anthropic announced that Dr. Christopher Stubbs, an experimental physicist and former Dean of Science at Harvard University, has joined its Long-Term Benefit Trust. He is the Trust's first physicist.

## Details

- **Background:** Samuel C. Moncher Professor of Physics and of Astronomy at Harvard; Dean of Science 2018 to 2024. He was a member of the High-Z Supernova Search Team that helped discover the accelerating expansion of the universe in 1998 (later awarded the Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics), and inaugural project scientist for what is now the Vera C. Rubin Observatory.
- **Other roles:** Member of JASON, the independent scientist group advising the US government since 1960; has advised on national security and arms control; has written on keeping AI-assisted research reproducible.
- **Current Trustees:** Stubbs joins Chair Neil Buddy Shah, Richard Fontaine and Ben Bernanke. Trustees are independent of management and investors.
- **Trust's role:** Anthropic is a Public Benefit Corporation; the Trust can appoint a majority of the board and advises the board and leadership on AI risks and societal impacts.
- **Context:** Anthropic published its first Public Benefit Report this week, and, subject to stockholder approval, committed to set aside a portion of shares for the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund.

## What happened next

This is a governance appointment with no product impact. It follows the week's Public Benefit Report and fund announcement, signalling Anthropic's emphasis on its public benefit structure. Terms of Stubbs's service were not disclosed.
