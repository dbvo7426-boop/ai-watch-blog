Anthropic, PBC announced on November 20, 2026 the closing of its initial public offering. This follows the November 10 roadshow, which gave an expected range of $70 to $78 per share for 825 million shares. The final price of $83.00 was above that range, and the share count was larger.

## Timeline

- **November 18:** the SEC declared the registration statement effective, and Anthropic priced the offering at $83.00 per share (980,000,000 Class A shares at pricing).
- **November 19:** Class A stock began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol ANT.
- **November 20:** the offering closed.

## Offering

- **Shares:** 1,127,000,000 Class A shares at $83.00, including the full exercise of the underwriters' option to buy 147,000,000 additional shares from Anthropic.
- **Sellers:** Anthropic sold 1,103,351,354 shares and certain selling stockholders sold 23,648,646. Anthropic received no proceeds from the stockholders' shares.
- **Gross proceeds:** about $93.5 billion before discounts, commissions and expenses, of which about $91.6 billion went to Anthropic.
- **Lead bookrunners:** Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley.

## Concurrent private placement

- 313,253,007 Class N common shares at $83.00, for about $26.0 billion.
- Buyers: AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA and TSMC Partners.
- The Class N shares were not registered under the Securities Act of 1933.

Combined gross proceeds from the IPO and the private placement are about $119.5 billion before underwriting discounts, commissions and expenses.
