OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT, now in beta, as a way for businesses to reach ChatGPT's users, which the article puts at 1.2 billion each week. It has two parts.

## Business Hub

- Businesses can claim their organization in ChatGPT. After verification, they can update key details and add self-service product feeds that include pricing and availability.
- Eligible businesses where ChatGPT is available can claim through Business Hub. Existing OpenAI Enterprise customers and advertisers work with their OpenAI representative instead.
- Shopify merchants can connect their store from Business Hub or the Shopify admin to speed up verification. Their eligible products are already integrated through Shopify Catalog.
- Aggregated insights show how people engage with a business, including mentions, website clicks and discovery topics, which are common themes in conversations that cite the business's website.
- OpenAI says it does not share users' conversations or Business Agent chats with businesses, and insights appear only when they meet minimum conversation or interaction thresholds.

## Business Agents

- Eligible businesses can build, test and launch a Business Agent that answers questions and helps people explore their offerings.
- When a business appears in a model response, users can open its ChatGPT profile and, if an agent is available, start a separate, clearly labeled conversation with it.
- Businesses can sponsor their Business Agent and use it as the destination for ChatGPT Ads. Sponsored placements are labeled and kept separate from ChatGPT responses.

## Availability

Business Agents are currently available only to eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the United States. OpenAI plans to expand to more markets and business categories and to build tools for organizations with more complex needs. Pricing is undisclosed.
