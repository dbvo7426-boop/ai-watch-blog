Google announced on November 18, 2026 that the Gemini app is available on eligible lower-memory Android (Go edition) devices. These phones previously offered only basic voice commands. Google says it re-architected the app so the core Gemini experience runs smoothly on hardware with less memory.

## Details

- **Gemini Live:** talk through problems or practice interviews aloud in more than 45 languages.
- **Camera:** point the camera at notes or forms to learn from and translate them.
- **Images:** create custom images with Nano Banana.
- **How to get it:** download from Google Play, or press and hold the power button on supported phones. It will also be preloaded on upcoming devices from partners such as Transsion.

The eligible device list is on a Google support page. Memory thresholds, countries and pricing were not disclosed in the post.
