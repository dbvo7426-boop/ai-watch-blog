LVMH Group's Media Division (Groupe Les Echos-Le Parisien) and OpenAI announced a strategic agreement on November 16 in Paris.

- OpenAI tools and ChatGPT Enterprise will be deployed to teams across the division so they can build AI applications for their daily work. Named uses include document research, document comparison and preliminary translation, plus personalisation of products for readers and advertisers.
- Content from the division's publications will appear more prominently in ChatGPT, with summaries and excerpts in answers to relevant questions, source identification and direct links to the original articles. Publications named include Les Echos, Le Parisien, Challenges, Paris Match, L'Opinion, Investir and L'Agefi.
- The rollout will include training tailored to each use case. Financial terms and rollout dates are undisclosed.
