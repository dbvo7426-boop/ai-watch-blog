Google announced Gemini 4 Neon on November 18, 2026, describing it as frontier-level intelligence with fast end-to-end task completion for software engineering, knowledge work and science. It sits between Gemini 4 Carbon (hardest long-horizon projects) and Gemini 4 Hydrogen (fast everyday execution).

## Pricing and specs

- $3 per million input tokens, $0.15 per million cached input tokens (95% discount), $15 per million output tokens.
- 1 million-token context window.

## Availability

- **Developers:** Gemini API paid tier in Google AI Studio and Android Studio; Pro and Ultra subscribers can also use it in AI Studio.
- **Enterprises:** Gemini for business (API or model dropdown) and Google Antigravity.
- **Consumers:** Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app.
- **Cyber defenders:** eligible Fairwind Program members get access over the following days.

## Benchmarks (reported by Google)

- Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index: 61 at xhigh thinking, #3 overall. Coding Agent Index: #1 (58.6).
- Terminal-Bench 4.1: 65.2%. Terminal-Bench Science: 70.5%. HLE-Diamond: 71.3%. RiemannBench: 84.0%.
- AutomationBench: #1 (56.3%). Vending-Bench 2: #1 ($16,843).
- Cybersecurity: AutoBaxBench hard tier 76%, WebExploitBench v1.2 51.26%, SEC-bench Pro 90.5%, CWE-bench v2 70.7%.

## Cost and safety

- At high thinking, Google says cost per task is 32% lower than Carbon and roughly 50% to 80% lower than top competing frontier models.
- Google reports 0.0% cheating on Terminal-Bench 4.0, a 0.09% misaligned action rate on its agentic safety suite and 0.0% attack success on Gray Swan's indirect prompt injection benchmark at the highest thinking level.

These figures are Google's own and have not been independently verified here.
