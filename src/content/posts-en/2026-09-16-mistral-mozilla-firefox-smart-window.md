Mistral AI and Mozilla announced a partnership on September 16, 2026 to bring "open, private and multilingual AI" to Firefox. Mistral's models now power Firefox Smart Window (beta), Mozilla's AI browsing assistant, starting with Mistral Small 4 for users in France and North America.

## Details

- **What Smart Window does**: helps users make sense of complex searches, recall information from tabs they've since closed, and pull together sourced answers based on what's open in the browser
- **Initial rollout**: Mistral Small 4 becomes available in the Smart Window beta for users in the United States, Canada, and France, with the United Kingdom and Germany expected to follow later in 2026
- **Not exclusive**: Mozilla says users will continue to be able to choose from a range of AI models inside Smart Window rather than being locked to Mistral alone
- **Privacy commitments**: conversations are not saved on Mozilla's servers by default, with zero data retention commitments cited as part of the partnership
- **Four stated pillars**: open distribution of the underlying technology, localization (models fine-tuned for regional languages and cultural context), user control and privacy, and extending sovereign, enterprise-grade AI to consumers
- **Leadership quotes**: Mozilla CEO Anthony Enzor-DeMeo said "a browser shouldn't be a one-way funnel," while Mistral co-founder Arthur Mensch framed the deal as bringing "privacy, control and choice to AI-powered web browsing"

## What happened next

The partnership builds on Mistral's push into consumer-facing distribution deals following its €3 billion Series D round earlier in September, and gives Mozilla a European, open-weight alternative to the US frontier labs already embedded in rival browsers. Firefox says the UK and Germany rollout will follow later this year, and that Smart Window will keep offering multiple model choices rather than settling on a single provider.
