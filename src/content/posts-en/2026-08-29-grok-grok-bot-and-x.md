xAI announced on August 29, 2026 that Grok Bot now integrates directly with X, letting users connect their X account from inside Grok Bot and immediately start delegating X-specific tasks to the always-on AI agent.

## Details

- **One-step connection**: users link their X account inside Grok Bot, and xAI automatically creates a developer account on their behalf if they don't already have one, removing a setup step that previously required a separate application process
- **Free API credits**: paying Grok Bot subscribers receive complimentary X API credits as part of the integration, rather than having to provision and pay for API access separately
- **Task coverage**: Grok Bot can now search posts, review a user's timeline, monitor mentions, and aggregate trending content on X as part of its broader "digital teammate" task set
- **Framed as a first step**: xAI describes this as an initial release and says it plans to keep expanding what Grok Bot can do on X

## What happened next

Access starts immediately for anyone who opens Grok Bot and signs in through the new X connector. The release lands about two and a half weeks after Grok Bot's early-beta debut and a few days after xAI widened the agent's availability across more SuperGrok and Cursor subscription tiers, continuing a rapid cadence of integrations meant to make Grok Bot useful across a user's existing tools rather than a standalone chat product.
