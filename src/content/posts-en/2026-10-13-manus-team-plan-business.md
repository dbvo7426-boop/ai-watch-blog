Manus has launched Manus for Business, a new line of team and enterprise offerings, with the Team Plan available immediately and an Enterprise Plan aimed at organizations with stricter compliance and control requirements.

## Details

- **Team Plan pricing**: starts at $20 per paid seat per month (or $200 per seat per year), including 4,000 credits, with usage scaling as needs grow; guest seats are free
- **Projects**: instructions, files, Skills, and connector settings now persist across related tasks, and scheduled tasks can reuse a Project's context for recurring work such as weekly reports
- **Team-wide Skills**: a workflow built by one person can be shared across the team, and admins can require a Skill be used when a process needs to stay consistent
- **Bounded collaboration**: teammates can contribute to shared tasks; a "Team Only" setting keeps tasks, replays, files, and internal sites inside the workspace, and free Guest users can review and comment on shared work without consuming a paid seat
- **Pooled credits**: credits from paid seats combine into a central, centrally billed pool with a team usage dashboard, and admins can top up credits as needed
- **Admin controls**: roles, sharing permissions, audit logs, SSO, and SCIM for managing workspace access
- **Enterprise Plan**: adds managed accounts, legal hold, SIEM log integration, agent network controls, and per-user usage limits, available via sales contact
- **Compliance**: Manus holds SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and ISO 27701 certifications, and says model providers are barred from training on Team customer data
- **Extras**: a free 60-minute AI audit session producing an implementation plan, and an in-person "Executive Dinners" event series

## What happened next

The launch extends Manus from an individual-agent product into a team collaboration platform, adding the organizational scaffolding — shared context, pooled billing, role-based access, and compliance certifications — that enterprise buyers typically require before adopting an AI tool company-wide. Combined with Manus's recent push into connectors, scheduled tasks, and sub-agent scaling, Manus for Business signals the company is now competing directly for enterprise software budgets rather than individual subscriptions.
