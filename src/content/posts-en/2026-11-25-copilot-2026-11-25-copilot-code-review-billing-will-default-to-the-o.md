GitHub announced that starting December 30, 2026, Copilot code review for members with a Copilot license will be billed to the organization by default, if no billing choice has been saved. The change applies to organizations and enterprises with Copilot Business or Copilot Enterprise.

**What changes**

- Today, when no choice is saved, reviews by members with a paid Copilot license use the member's Copilot entitlement.
- From December 30, 2026, those reviews will be billed to the organization that owns the repository.
- Accounts that do not have AI Credits paid usage enabled, have a billing lock, or have no billable customer will be set to **Member** instead.
- If you already chose **Member** or **Organization**, your choice stays in effect.
- The separate setting for members without a Copilot license does not change.

**How to keep member billing**

Until the change, unset settings show **Default (Member)** with a notice about the upcoming change. To keep using members' Copilot entitlements, select **Member** in your enterprise or organization Copilot policy settings before December 30, 2026. That choice remains after the default changes. If an enterprise manages this setting, an enterprise owner makes the choice.

**Cost note:** organization billing requires AI Credits paid usage and can increase the organization's spending. Specific prices were not disclosed in the announcement.

More details are in GitHub's documentation, "Configuring code review by GitHub Copilot".
