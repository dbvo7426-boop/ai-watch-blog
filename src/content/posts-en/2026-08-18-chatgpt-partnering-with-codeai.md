OpenAI announced a "signature partnership" with CodeAI, an education organization led by CEO Karim Meghji, to give students and educators access to AI-literacy tools and resources. The announcement is timed to coincide with the launch of ChatGPT for Teens.

## Details

- **What CodeAI brings**: CodeAI develops "AI Foundations," described as a free, year-long high school course, and has previously run its own student surveys on AI use
- **Named program elements**: an "Hour of AI" program that OpenAI says aims to reach millions of students; a "Builders Challenge" offering mentorship and a national showcase for high schoolers; curriculum support for AI Foundations from OpenAI staff; a "Career Journeys" series featuring OpenAI employees discussing their work; and an advisory council combining experts in child development, youth policy, and learning science
- **What's not disclosed**: OpenAI's announcement does not give funding amounts, a target number of schools or students, specific launch dates for the individual programs beyond "over the next year," or any measurable outcomes or success metrics
- **Framing**: The partnership is presented as part of OpenAI's broader push to position ChatGPT as a tool for education, running alongside the ChatGPT for Teens launch rather than as a standalone product announcement

## What happened next

This reads as a framework announcement — it names several programs and an advisory structure but provides little of the operational detail (funding, scale, timing) that would let outsiders assess its real reach. It functions largely as supporting context for the same-day ChatGPT for Teens launch, giving OpenAI an education-sector partner to point to alongside the new product's safety and learning features.
