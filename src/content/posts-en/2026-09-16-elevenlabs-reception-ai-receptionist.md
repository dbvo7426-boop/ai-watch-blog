ElevenLabs launched Reception on September 16, 2026, a new product under its ElevenAgents line that acts as an always-on AI receptionist for small businesses, answering phone calls and booking appointments without requiring any technical setup.

## Details

- **24/7 call handling**: Reception answers incoming calls around the clock, including when staff are busy or after hours, so businesses stop missing calls
- **Multilingual Q&A**: it responds to caller questions about services, hours, and location in multiple languages
- **Real calendar integration**: it books appointments by checking actual staff availability and syncing with existing calendars, rather than just taking messages
- **Follow-up materials**: teams get call summaries, transcripts, and recordings for every interaction
- **Flexible phone setup**: works with a business's existing phone line or a new dedicated number
- **Booking page included**: comes with a public-facing online booking page for customers
- **Target industries**: ElevenLabs is pitching Reception at home services (plumbing, electrical, HVAC), professional and consulting services, real estate and property management, and salons/studios
- **No-code setup**: ElevenLabs says "setup takes minutes and requires no technical experience," with pricing detailed at reception.ai/pricing

## What happened next

Reception extends ElevenLabs' voice-agent business beyond enterprise and developer-facing tools into a packaged, self-serve product aimed squarely at small and local businesses — a market segment the company had not directly targeted with a dedicated branded product before. It caps off a five-day run of ElevenLabs product news that also included the Music v2.5 model and the MCP creative-generation expansion.
