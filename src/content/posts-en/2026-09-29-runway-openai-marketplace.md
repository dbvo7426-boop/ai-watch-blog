Runway announced on September 29, 2026 that it has joined the OpenAI Marketplace as a launch partner, making Runway Creative available to enterprise buyers who can now apply their existing OpenAI spending commitments toward Runway purchases.

## Details

- **New distribution channel**: Runway Creative is now listed in the OpenAI Marketplace Directory, giving enterprise teams a direct path to procure Runway's tools through commitments they already have with OpenAI
- **Model lineup included**: the bundle covers Runway's own Gen-4.5 alongside Seedance 2.5, GPT Image 2.5, and ElevenLabs V4, spanning video, image, audio, and editing generation
- **Core capabilities**: teams can generate video and images from text, images, or existing footage, make edits through natural-language instructions, and use Runway Agent to plan, generate, and assemble full videos conversationally
- **Scale claim**: Runway says more than 60 million creators, filmmakers, and marketing teams already use the platform, with enterprise customers including Amazon, Robinhood, Dolce & Gabbana, and Microsoft
- **How to access**: interested enterprise teams can reach Runway's sales team through the marketplace listing or the OpenAI business marketplace

## What happened next

The listing goes live immediately in the OpenAI Marketplace Directory, giving large organizations a lower-friction way to adopt Runway's creative suite without separate procurement. The move follows Runway's "Runway for Adobe" integration announced earlier in September, continuing a pattern of Runway embedding itself into the software and purchasing workflows enterprises already use rather than competing purely on a standalone app.
