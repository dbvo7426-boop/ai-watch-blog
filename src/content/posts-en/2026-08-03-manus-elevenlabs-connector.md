Manus has added an ElevenLabs connector, bringing audio generation, transcription, and voice cloning directly into its conversational workflow. Users can complete audio tasks by simply describing what they need in chat, without managing API keys or writing code.

## Details

- **Generate**: Create audio content from text descriptions — Manus drafts scripts and produces finished files using the user's connected ElevenLabs account. Examples cited include turning a newsletter into a podcast, creating product demo voiceovers in multiple languages, and designing soundscapes
- **Process**: Upload and analyze existing audio recordings — the connector transcribes content, identifies speakers, summarizes insights, and extracts key information, useful for things like customer interviews or meeting recordings
- **Build**: Develop voice-powered applications — users can ask Manus to build full apps, such as song generators or language tutors, that use ElevenLabs as the audio backbone
- **No setup required**: Available features depend on the user's ElevenLabs plan and credit balance, and ElevenLabs itself handles billing and data retention independently of Manus

## How to try it

- In Manus, go to Integrations, find ElevenLabs, click Connect, sign in, and authorize access — no API key management needed
- Requires an active ElevenLabs account
- Manus recommends reviewing all AI-generated audio before publishing it
