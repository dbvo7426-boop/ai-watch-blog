Gemini 4 Neon is generally available in GitHub Copilot, with a gradual rollout. It is designed for agentic coding, multistep tool use and knowledge work. According to Google, it delivers quality close to Gemini 4 Carbon at a lower token cost per task, starts responding faster, makes fewer unrequested changes and is less likely to make unsupported claims about code, tools or test results.

**Plans:** Copilot Pro+, Max, Business and Enterprise.

**Where:** the model picker in Visual Studio Code, Visual Studio, Copilot CLI, the Copilot coding agent, the Copilot app, github.com, GitHub Mobile (iOS and Android), JetBrains IDEs, Xcode and Eclipse.

**Billing and admin:** usage-based billing at provider list pricing; no multiplier is listed. Business and Enterprise admins control access through the model policy in Copilot settings; default model enablement turns it on unless the global default or the model was disabled.
