Claude Meetings is rolling out in beta on Team and Enterprise plans, in Claude and Claude Desktop.

## Details

- **What it does:** Claude can join a Google Meet call as a visible participant. During the call it can take notes, answer questions, and create artifacts such as docs, whiteboards and slides.
- **Availability:** beta, Team and Enterprise plans only.
- **Not described:** requirements beyond the plan, limits and pricing are undisclosed in the release note. Setup steps are in the Use Claude Meetings help article (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18325154-use-claude-meetings).
