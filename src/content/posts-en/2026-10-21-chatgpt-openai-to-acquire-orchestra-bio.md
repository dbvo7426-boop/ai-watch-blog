OpenAI announced it is acquiring Orchestra Bio, whose platform helps biopharma teams coordinate drug development. Orchestra's team and technology will be combined with Rosalind, OpenAI's models and tools for life sciences research.

## Details

- **Orchestra platform:** people and AI agents work together, with agents monitoring and tracking studies and campaigns and identifying which activities and timelines a problem affects, such as a failed manufacturing batch delaying a clinical trial.
- **Rosalind:** GPT-Rosalind helps scientists reason through biology and chemistry questions; Rosalind Workbench connects these capabilities with scientific tools and data.
- **Context:** OpenAI cites a 10 to 15 year average to develop a medicine, and existing partnerships with Novo Nordisk and Moderna.
- **Undisclosed:** deal price, terms and closing timeline were not given in the source.

## What happened next

The deal extends OpenAI from research assistance into R&D operations and program management for pharma. Integration details and product timing remain to be announced.
