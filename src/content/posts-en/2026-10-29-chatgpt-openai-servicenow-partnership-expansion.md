OpenAI and ServiceNow are expanding the partnership first announced in January, bringing ServiceNow business context and workflow actions into ChatGPT and adding OpenAI models and Codex to ServiceNow products.

## Details

- **ChatGPT integration:** Users can find similar incidents, assess change risk and update ServiceNow records without leaving ChatGPT. Customers connect ChatGPT to their own ServiceNow instance and choose which capabilities to enable; ServiceNow Action Fabric enforces existing business rules and permissions.
- **Application development:** A planned Codex integration in ServiceNow AI Workflow Factory, and OpenAI models powering Build Agent and Autonomous Engineer.
- **Cybersecurity:** A joint solution combining OpenAI security capabilities with ServiceNow asset, identity and business context to investigate vulnerabilities and coordinate remediation.
- **Voice:** An OpenAI-powered feature in the Super Intelligence Control Tower that lets users describe an AI use case verbally and fills in assessment fields.
- **Internal use:** ServiceNow plans to give all employees access to ChatGPT Work and Codex; OpenAI will use ServiceNow workflows internally.
- **Timeline:** ServiceNow MCP plugin available now in the OpenAI marketplace; enhanced MCP capabilities by end of 2026; security early access January 2027 and GA Q2 2027; voice Q1 2027; Codex in AI Workflow Factory in 2027.

## What happened next

Only the MCP plugin is shipping today; most of the rest is a roadmap running into 2027. Pricing and customer numbers are undisclosed. The practical takeaway is that ChatGPT is becoming a front end for enterprise IT workflows via MCP.
