Mistral AI has added version control for AI prompts and skills to its Studio platform. Most enterprises today have prompts and skills scattered and unmanaged, leading to inconsistent behavior and issues that are hard to trace — Studio now aims to be the "system of record" for these assets, tracked, owned, and traceable.

## Details

- **Versioning**: Every prompt and skill is tracked as a managed asset with an owner, full history, and lineage. A version that has shipped can't be quietly changed afterward, so the record always matches what actually ran
- **Compare and revert**: Any two versions can be compared to see exactly what changed, and reverting to a known-good version takes minutes
- **Audit logs**: Every change is logged by default with who made it and when, providing the audit trail auditors ask for
- **Ownership**: Every asset has a named owner, making accountability for changes explicit
- **Classification labels**: Labels such as "Production" or "Staging" make it easy to find and organize the right prompts and skills

## How to try it

- Available now as part of Mistral AI Studio for existing Studio users
- No separate pricing for this capability was specified
