Lovable's changelog lists several product changes between November 9 and 12, 2026. The Automations entry is dated November 11.

## Automations (Nov 11)

You can ask for recurring tasks in Chats, such as a morning check of published sites or a weekly signup summary. Lovable posts the results in that chat. Automations are available on Free, Pro and Business plans, and each run counts as chat usage.

## Personal settings and Files (Nov 10)

- **Personal skills:** Playbooks that only you can use, with a separate set in each workspace.
- **Personal instructions:** Rules and preferences that apply to every project you open in a workspace. Teammates cannot see them.
- **Personal memory:** Memory is now separate for each workspace and has moved to account settings.
- **Image tools in Files:** Resize an image to a new shape, extending the scene rather than cropping it, or animate it into a short video. Results are saved as new files.
- **Admin:** Enterprise admins can require two-factor authentication from external collaborators only, and Business and Enterprise admins can change several connectors' availability at once.

## Connectors and alerts

- **Nov 9:** Jira and Mailjet connectors, the Sentry connector inside apps, and sign-in with a Resend account.
- **Nov 12:** Deel and Retool connectors, plus run usage alerts for workspace admins and owners on paid plans when daily run usage rises sharply compared with the previous 7 days.
- **Nov 11:** Workspace and project invitations are no longer accepted automatically, and the Auto-accept invitations setting was removed.
