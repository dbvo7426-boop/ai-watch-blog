Google is offering college students a free year of its paid Gemini plans to start the school semester, with different tiers depending on region.

## Details

- **US students**: Get Google AI Pro (normally $19.99/month) free for 12 months, including 4x higher Gemini usage limits, access to Gemini Spark, Gemini integration with Gmail and Docs, 5TB of storage, and Google Health Premium
- **International students (140+ markets)**: Get Google AI Plus (normally about $4.99/month locally) free for 12 months, including access to Gemini Omni, 2x higher usage limits, and 400GB of storage
- **Bundle option**: In select markets, students can add YouTube Premium bundled with Google AI Pro at up to 70% off
- **Study features included**: Study notebooks with syllabus organization, interactive 3D visualizations, Deep Research in Gemini Live, and calendar integration for deadlines
- **Eligibility and deadline**: Open to college students, with some regions excluded (including Bolivia, Albania, Canada, Macau, Hong Kong, and Tunisia); must redeem by December 31, 2026; a valid payment method is required at signup and the plan auto-renews at the standard rate after the free year

## How to try it

- US students can sign up at one.google.com/ai-student
- All eligible students can also start from the new student hub at gemini.google.com/students
